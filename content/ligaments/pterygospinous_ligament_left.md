The pterygospinous ligament is a thin band, sometimes ossified, running from the spine of the sphenoid bone to the posterior border of the lateral pterygoid plate. When present it can convert the gap between them into a foramen. It lies near the mandibular nerve in the infratemporal fossa and is a variable structure rather than a constant one.
