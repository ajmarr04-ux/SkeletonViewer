The tibia is the larger, medial bone of the leg and the one that bears the weight. Its broad upper end has two flat condyles that carry the femur at the knee. The shaft is triangular, with a sharp anterior border, the shin, felt under the skin. Below, it articulates with the talus at the ankle and forms the medial malleolus.
