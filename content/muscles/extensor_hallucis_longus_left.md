Extensor hallucis longus is a thin muscle hidden between tibialis anterior and extensor digitorum longus. It arises from the middle of the medial surface of the fibula and the interosseous membrane, and its tendon crosses the front of the ankle to the base of the distal phalanx of the big toe. It extends the big toe and helps dorsiflex the ankle.
