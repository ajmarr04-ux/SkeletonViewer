The dorsal metacarpal ligaments are transverse bands on the back of the hand that connect the dorsal surfaces of the bases of neighbouring metacarpals, from the second to the fifth. They reinforce the intermetacarpal joints and help to hold the metacarpal bases together as a stable row.
