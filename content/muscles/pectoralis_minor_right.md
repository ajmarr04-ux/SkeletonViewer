Pectoralis minor is a thin, triangular muscle deep to pectoralis major. It arises from the outer surfaces of the third to fifth ribs near their costal cartilages and converges upwards and laterally to the medial border and upper surface of the coracoid process. It steadies the scapula by drawing it forwards and down against the chest wall, and raises the ribs in forced inspiration when the scapula is fixed.
