The patellar ligament is a strong, flat band that continues the quadriceps tendon below the kneecap. It runs from the apex and lower border of the patella to the tibial tuberosity. It transmits the pull of the quadriceps femoris to the tibia, so it carries the force that extends the knee, and it is easily felt in front of the joint when the knee is bent.
