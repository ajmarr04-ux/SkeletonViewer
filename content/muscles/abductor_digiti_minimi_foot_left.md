Abductor digiti minimi forms the lateral border of the sole. It arises from both processes of the calcaneal tuberosity and the plantar aponeurosis, and runs along the outer edge of the foot to the lateral side of the base of the proximal phalanx of the little toe. It abducts and helps flex the little toe and supports the lateral arch.
