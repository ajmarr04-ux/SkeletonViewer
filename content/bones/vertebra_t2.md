The second thoracic vertebra is a typical thoracic vertebra, with a heart-shaped body, a small round vertebral foramen and a long spinous process sloping down over the vertebra below. Demifacets on its body take the heads of ribs 2 and 3, and the facet on each transverse process takes the tubercle of rib 2.
