The seventh rib is the longest and the last true rib, the last whose own costal cartilage reaches the sternum. Its head articulates with T6 and T7 and its tubercle with the transverse process of T7. Its cartilage, at the lower end of the sternum, also anchors the diaphragm and abdominal muscles.
