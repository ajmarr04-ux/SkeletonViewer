The stylohyoid ligament is a fibrous cord running from the tip of the styloid process of the temporal bone to the lesser horn of the hyoid bone. It is a remnant of the second pharyngeal arch cartilage. It helps to suspend the hyoid and may contain cartilage or bone in places. It lies close to the stylohyoid muscle and the palatine tonsil.
