The proximal phalanx of the third toe is a short, slender long bone. Its base articulates with the head of the third metatarsal, and its pulley-shaped head with the middle phalanx at the proximal interphalangeal joint. Interosseous muscles insert on its base, and the long and short flexor tendons run along its sole surface in a fibrous sheath.
