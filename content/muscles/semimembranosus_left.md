Semimembranosus is the deeper medial hamstring, named for the flat membranous tendon at its origin on the ischial tuberosity. It lies beneath semitendinosus and inserts mainly on the back of the medial condyle of the tibia, with an expansion forming the oblique popliteal ligament of the knee. It extends the hip, flexes the knee and medially rotates the flexed knee.
