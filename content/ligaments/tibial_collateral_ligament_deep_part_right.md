The deep part of the tibial collateral ligament is a short thickening of the medial joint capsule beneath the superficial part. It is divided into meniscofemoral and meniscotibial parts, which run from the medial femoral condyle and the medial tibial condyle to the rim of the medial meniscus. It firmly attaches the medial meniscus to both bones.
