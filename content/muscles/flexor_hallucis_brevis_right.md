Flexor hallucis brevis lies under the first metatarsal in the third layer of the sole. It arises from the plantar surfaces of the cuboid and lateral cuneiform and divides into medial and lateral heads, which insert on either side of the base of the proximal phalanx of the big toe, each through a sesamoid bone. It flexes the big toe at the metatarsophalangeal joint.
