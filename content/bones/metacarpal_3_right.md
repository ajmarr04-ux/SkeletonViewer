The third metacarpal, in the middle finger, has a small styloid process projecting from the back of its base. Its base articulates with the capitate and with the second and fourth metacarpals, so it is held almost still and forms the central axis of the hand. Extensor carpi radialis brevis inserts on its base, and the transverse head of adductor pollicis arises from its shaft.
