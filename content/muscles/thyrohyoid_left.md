Thyrohyoid is a small quadrilateral muscle that looks like the upward continuation of sternothyroid. It runs from the oblique line of the thyroid cartilage to the lower border of the greater horn and body of the hyoid. With the hyoid fixed it raises the larynx, as in swallowing and singing high notes; with the larynx fixed it depresses the hyoid.
