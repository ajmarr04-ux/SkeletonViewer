The middle glenohumeral ligament is a thickening of the front of the shoulder capsule, lying below the superior ligament. It runs from the anterior glenoid margin and labrum of the scapula to the lesser tubercle region of the humerus. It tightens as the arm rotates outwards, and so helps to stop the humeral head sliding forwards. Its size varies between people.
