Abductor digiti minimi is the most superficial and medial muscle of the hypothenar eminence. It arises from the pisiform and the tendon of flexor carpi ulnaris and inserts on the medial side of the base of the proximal phalanx of the little finger and its extensor expansion. It abducts the little finger, spreading it away from the ring finger, and helps flex its knuckle.
