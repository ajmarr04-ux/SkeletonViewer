The distal phalanx of the index finger forms the fingertip. Its base articulates with the middle phalanx at the distal interphalangeal joint, and its flattened end widens into a rough tuberosity that supports the pulp and the nail. Flexor digitorum profundus inserts on its palm surface and is the only muscle that bends the fingertip.
