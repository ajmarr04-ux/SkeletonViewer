The mandible is the largest and strongest bone of the face and the only skull bone that moves freely. Its horseshoe-shaped body holds the lower teeth, and its two rami rise to the condyles, which form the temporomandibular joints with the temporal bones. The muscles of chewing move it in eating and speech.
