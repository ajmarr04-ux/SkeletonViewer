The coracohumeral ligament is a broad band that passes from the lateral border of the coracoid process of the scapula to the greater and lesser tubercles of the humerus. It blends with the upper part of the shoulder capsule. It helps to support the weight of the hanging arm and restrains the humeral head from moving downwards and rotating outwards.
