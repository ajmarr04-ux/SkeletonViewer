The temporal bone forms part of the side and base of the skull. Its dense petrous part houses the cochlea, vestibule and semicircular canals, and its middle-ear cavity holds the ossicles. The mandibular fossa forms the temporomandibular joint, and the mastoid process behind the ear anchors the sternocleidomastoid muscle.
