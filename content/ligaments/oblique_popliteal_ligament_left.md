The oblique popliteal ligament is a broad, flat band on the back of the knee. It is mainly an expansion of the tendon of semimembranosus, arising near the medial tibial condyle and passing upwards and laterally across the joint capsule to the lateral femoral condyle and the intercondylar line. It strengthens the posterior capsule and helps to resist hyperextension.
