Flexor digiti minimi brevis is a small muscle under the fifth metatarsal, in the third layer of the sole. It arises from the base of the fifth metatarsal and the sheath of the fibularis longus tendon, and inserts on the lateral side of the base of the proximal phalanx of the little toe, which it flexes at the metatarsophalangeal joint.
