Semitendinosus is a medial hamstring, named for its long tendon, which makes up nearly half its length. It arises from the ischial tuberosity with the long head of biceps femoris and inserts on the upper medial surface of the tibia, behind gracilis in the pes anserinus. It extends the hip, flexes the knee and medially rotates the flexed knee.
