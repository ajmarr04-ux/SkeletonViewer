Tibialis posterior is the deepest muscle at the back of the leg. It arises from the interosseous membrane and the adjoining back of the tibia and fibula, and its tendon passes behind the medial malleolus to the navicular tuberosity, with slips to most of the other tarsal bones and the middle metatarsals. It inverts the foot, helps plantarflex the ankle and supports the medial arch.
