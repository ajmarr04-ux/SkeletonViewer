The distal phalanx of the big toe is the end bone of the big toe. Its broad base articulates with the proximal phalanx at the interphalangeal joint, and its tip flattens into a tuberosity that supports the nail and the pulp. Flexor hallucis longus inserts on its sole surface and extensor hallucis longus on its back.
