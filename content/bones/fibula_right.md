The fibula is the slender, lateral bone of the leg. Its head articulates with the tibia just below the knee, outside the knee joint, and the two bones are bound together at the ankle. Its lower end forms the lateral malleolus, which grips the side of the talus. It carries little weight but anchors many leg muscles.
