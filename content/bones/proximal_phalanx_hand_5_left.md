The proximal phalanx of the little finger is the shortest of the four finger proximal phalanges. Its base articulates with the head of the fifth metacarpal, and its head with the middle phalanx. The hypothenar muscles, abductor digiti minimi and flexor digiti minimi brevis, insert on the ulnar side of its base and spread the finger away from the hand.
