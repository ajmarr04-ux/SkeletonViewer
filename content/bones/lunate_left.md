The lunate is a crescent-shaped bone in the middle of the proximal carpal row. Its convex upper surface articulates with the radius at the wrist, and its concave lower surface cups the head of the capitate. It also articulates with the scaphoid and triquetrum at its sides and, in most people, the hamate. No muscles attach to it.
