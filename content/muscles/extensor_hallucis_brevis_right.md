Extensor hallucis brevis is the medial slip of the short extensor on the top of the foot, often described as part of extensor digitorum brevis. It arises from the upper surface of the calcaneus and runs forwards and medially to the dorsal surface of the base of the proximal phalanx of the big toe, which it helps extend at the metatarsophalangeal joint.
