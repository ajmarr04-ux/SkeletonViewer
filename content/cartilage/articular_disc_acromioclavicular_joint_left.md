The articular disc of the acromioclavicular joint is a thin wedge of fibrocartilage that partly divides the joint between the acromial end of the clavicle and the acromion of the scapula. It is often incomplete and may be absent, and it becomes worn with age. It adjusts the fit between the two bony surfaces and cushions the joint.
