The dorsal cuneocuboid ligament is a transverse band on the upper surface of the midfoot. It connects the dorsal surface of the cuboid to that of the lateral cuneiform. It strengthens the dorsal part of the cuneocuboid joint and holds the two bones together, helping to maintain the transverse arch of the foot.
