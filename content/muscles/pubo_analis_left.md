Pubo-analis is a medial part of levator ani. Its fibres arise from the back of the body of the pubis beside pubococcygeus and pass backwards to blend with the wall of the anal canal, between the internal and external sphincters, and with the perineal body. It helps support the pelvic viscera and, with puborectalis, contributes to faecal continence.
