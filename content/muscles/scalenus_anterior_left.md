Scalenus anterior lies deep to sternocleidomastoid at the side of the neck. It arises from the anterior tubercles of the transverse processes of C3 to C6 and descends almost vertically to the scalene tubercle on the inner border of the first rib. It raises the first rib in forced inspiration and bends the neck forwards and to its own side. It is a key landmark of the root of the neck.
