Bucinator forms the muscular wall of the cheek. It arises from the outer surfaces of the alveolar processes of the maxilla and mandible opposite the molar teeth and from the pterygomandibular raphe, and runs forwards to blend with orbicularis oris at the angle of the mouth. It presses the cheek against the teeth, keeping food between them while chewing, and expels air when blowing.
