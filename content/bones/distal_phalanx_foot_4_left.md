The distal phalanx of the fourth toe is the end bone of that toe. Its base articulates with the middle phalanx, and its rough, flattened tuberosity supports the toenail and the pulp. Flexor digitorum longus inserts on its sole surface, and the long and short extensor tendons on its back.
