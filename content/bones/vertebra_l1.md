The first lumbar vertebra has the large, kidney-shaped body typical of the lumbar spine, thick pedicles and laminae, and a broad, square spinous process. Its facet joints face inwards and backwards, allowing bending but little rotation. The spinal cord usually ends near its lower border, where the cauda equina begins.
