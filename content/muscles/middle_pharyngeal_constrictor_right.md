The middle pharyngeal constrictor is a fan-shaped sheet arising from the greater and lesser horns of the hyoid and the lower end of the stylohyoid ligament. Its fibres spread backwards around the pharynx to the median raphe, overlapping the superior constrictor above and overlapped by the inferior constrictor below. It constricts the middle of the pharynx in swallowing.
