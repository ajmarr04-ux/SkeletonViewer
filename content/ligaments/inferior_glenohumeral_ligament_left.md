The inferior glenohumeral ligament is a thickening of the lower front of the shoulder capsule. It is attached to the glenoid labrum and edge of the glenoid cavity of the scapula and to the anatomical neck of the humerus. Its anterior and posterior bands form a sling beneath the humeral head that tightens when the arm is abducted and rotated outwards.
