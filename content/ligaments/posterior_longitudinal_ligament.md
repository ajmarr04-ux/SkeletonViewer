The posterior longitudinal ligament runs inside the vertebral canal along the back of the vertebral bodies, from the body of the axis, where it continues the tectorial membrane, down to the sacrum. It is narrow over the vertebral bodies but widens over the intervertebral discs, to which it is attached. It resists flexion and backward disc protrusion.
