Extensor digitorum longus is a feather-shaped muscle on the lateral side of the front of the leg. It arises from the lateral condyle of the tibia, the fibula and the interosseous membrane, and its tendon divides into four on the front of the ankle. They form the extensor expansions of the second to fifth toes, which they extend, and the muscle helps dorsiflex the ankle.
