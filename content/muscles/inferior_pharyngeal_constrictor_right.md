The inferior pharyngeal constrictor is the thickest of the three. Its thyropharyngeal part arises from the oblique line of the thyroid cartilage and inserts into the pharyngeal raphe; its cricopharyngeal part, from the side of the cricoid cartilage, encircles the pharyngo-oesophageal junction without a raphe and acts as the upper oesophageal sphincter.
