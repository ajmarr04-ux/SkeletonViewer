Depressor septi nasi is a small muscle that arises from the incisive fossa of the maxilla, above the central incisor, deep to orbicularis oris, and passes upwards into the mobile part of the nasal septum and the back of the ala. It pulls the tip of the nose and the septum downwards and helps the alar part of nasalis widen the nostril in deep breathing.
