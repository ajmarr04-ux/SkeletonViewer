Extensor digiti minimi is a slender muscle, often partly joined to extensor digitorum, on the ulnar side of the back of the forearm. It arises from the common extensor tendon and passes through its own compartment of the extensor retinaculum, joining the extensor expansion of the little finger. It extends the little finger, allowing it to be raised on its own.
