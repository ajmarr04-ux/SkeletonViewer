The sacrotuberous ligament is a strong, fan-shaped band that runs from the posterior iliac spines, the lower sacrum and the coccyx to the ischial tuberosity. Its fibres twist as they descend, and its lower end spreads onto the ischium. It helps to stabilise the sacrum against forward tilting and bounds the sciatic foramina.
