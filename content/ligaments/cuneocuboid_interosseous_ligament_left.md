The cuneocuboid interosseous ligament is a strong, short band that lies deep between the lateral cuneiform and the cuboid. It joins the adjoining non-articular parts of their facing surfaces, behind the cuneocuboid joint. It binds the two bones tightly together and so limits movement between them, helping to keep the transverse arch of the midfoot stable.
