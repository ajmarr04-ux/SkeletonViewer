Lateral crico-arytenoid is a small muscle that runs from the upper border of the arch of the cricoid cartilage backwards and upwards to the front of the muscular process of the arytenoid cartilage. It rotates the arytenoid so that its vocal process swings medially, bringing the vocal folds together and closing the front of the rima glottidis, as in phonation.
