Extensor pollicis brevis arises from the back of the radius and the interosseous membrane, below abductor pollicis longus, and runs with it in the first dorsal compartment at the wrist. Its tendon forms the front border of the anatomical snuffbox and inserts on the base of the proximal phalanx of the thumb. It extends the metacarpophalangeal joint of the thumb and helps extend the carpometacarpal joint.
