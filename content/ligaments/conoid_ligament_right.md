The conoid ligament is the medial, cone-shaped part of the coracoclavicular ligament. Its apex lies on the root of the coracoid process of the scapula, and its broad base attaches to the conoid tubercle on the underside of the clavicle. It helps to carry the weight of the arm from the clavicle and to keep the clavicle and scapula linked.
