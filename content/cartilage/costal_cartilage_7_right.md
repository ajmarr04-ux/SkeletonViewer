The seventh costal cartilage is the longest of the true-rib cartilages. It continues the front end of the seventh rib and runs medially and upwards to the lower end of the body of the sternum, near the xiphisternal junction, forming a synovial sternocostal joint. Its lower border meets the cartilage of the eighth rib.
