The intercornual ligament is a short band on the left side of the sacrococcygeal joint. It connects the sacral cornu, the small projection at the lower end of the sacral canal, to the coccygeal cornu, completing the foramen for the fifth sacral nerve. It reinforces the joint at the side, where it is continuous with the other ligaments of the sacrococcygeal region.
