The dorsal metatarsal ligaments are short transverse bands on the upper surface of the foot. They connect the dorsal surfaces of the bases of neighbouring metatarsal bones, running across the intermetatarsal joints. They bind the metatarsal bases together and help to keep the forefoot arch stable.
