The fifth cervical vertebra is a typical cervical vertebra, with a broad, short body, uncinate processes, a large triangular vertebral foramen and a bifid spinous process. It sits in the most mobile part of the neck, so the discs and facet joints around it bear a great deal of bending and wear.
