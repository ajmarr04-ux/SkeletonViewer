The palmar radio-ulnar ligament is a band on the front of the wrist that strengthens the capsule of the distal radio-ulnar joint. It passes between the anterior margin of the ulnar notch of the radius and the head and styloid region of the ulna. It keeps the lower ends of the two bones together during pronation and supination.
