Posterior crico-arytenoid is a fan-shaped muscle on the back of the cricoid lamina, beside its fellow in the midline. Its fibres converge upwards and laterally on the muscular process of the arytenoid cartilage. It rotates the arytenoid so that its vocal process swings laterally, opening the rima glottidis. It is the only muscle that abducts the vocal folds.
