The scaphotrapeziotrapezoidal ligament is a strong palmar ligament running from the distal end of the scaphoid, including its tubercle, to the trapezium and trapezoid. It braces the junction between the scaphoid and the two bones of the distal row, and it helps to stabilise the scaphoid as it moves during wrist motion.
