The superior pharyngeal constrictor is a thin, quadrilateral sheet forming the upper part of the pharyngeal wall. It arises from the pterygoid hamulus, the pterygomandibular raphe, the back of the mylohyoid line and the side of the tongue, and curves back to the median pharyngeal raphe and the pharyngeal tubercle of the occipital bone. In swallowing it contracts in sequence with the others, propelling food downwards.
