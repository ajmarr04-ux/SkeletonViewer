Stylopharyngeus is a long, slender muscle arising from the medial side of the base of the styloid process. It descends between the superior and middle pharyngeal constrictors and blends with palatopharyngeus inside the pharyngeal wall, reaching the thyroid cartilage. It raises the pharynx and larynx and widens the pharynx in swallowing. It is the only muscle supplied by the glossopharyngeal nerve.
