Levator nasolabialis, better known as levator labii superioris alaeque nasi, arises from the upper part of the frontal process of the maxilla beside the nose. It splits into a medial slip to the greater alar cartilage and skin of the nose and a lateral slip into the upper lip. It raises the upper lip and dilates the nostril, as in a snarl or sneer.
