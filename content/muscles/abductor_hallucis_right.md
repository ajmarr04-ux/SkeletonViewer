Abductor hallucis lies along the medial border of the sole, forming the bulge in front of the heel on the inner side of the foot. It arises from the medial process of the calcaneal tuberosity, the flexor retinaculum and the plantar aponeurosis, and inserts on the medial side of the base of the proximal phalanx of the big toe. It abducts and helps flex the big toe and supports the medial arch.
