Splenius colli (splenius cervicis) is the narrower, lower continuation of splenius capitis. It arises from the spinous processes of T3 to T6 and ascends to the posterior tubercles of the transverse processes of the upper two or three cervical vertebrae, deep to levator scapulae. It extends the neck and, acting on one side, bends it and turns it to the same side.
