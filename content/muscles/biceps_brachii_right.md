Biceps brachii is the prominent two-headed muscle on the front of the arm. Its long and short heads arise from the scapula and join in a belly that inserts on the radial tuberosity, with an aponeurosis fanning into the forearm fascia. It is the most powerful supinator of the forearm and a strong flexor of the elbow, especially with the forearm supinated, and it also helps flex the shoulder.
