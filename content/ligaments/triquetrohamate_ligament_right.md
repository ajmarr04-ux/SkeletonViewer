The triquetrohamate ligament is a short intercarpal ligament joining the triquetrum to the hamate on the ulnar side of the wrist. It crosses the midcarpal joint and ties the proximal row to the distal row, helping to stabilise the ulnar side of the carpus during movements of the wrist.
