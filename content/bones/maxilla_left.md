The two maxillae form the upper jaw, meeting in the midline below the nose. Each holds the upper teeth in its alveolar process and forms most of the hard palate, the floor of the orbit and the side wall of the nasal cavity. Its body contains the maxillary sinus, the largest paranasal sinus.
