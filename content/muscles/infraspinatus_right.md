Infraspinatus is a thick, triangular muscle that fills most of the infraspinous fossa on the back of the scapula. Its tendon crosses the back of the shoulder joint, blending with the capsule, to the middle facet of the greater tubercle of the humerus. It is the main lateral rotator of the arm and, as part of the rotator cuff, steadies the head of the humerus.
