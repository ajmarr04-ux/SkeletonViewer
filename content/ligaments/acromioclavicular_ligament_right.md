The acromioclavicular ligament is a band of fibres in the capsule of the acromioclavicular joint. It runs from the upper surface of the acromial end of the clavicle to the upper surface of the acromion of the scapula. It controls small horizontal movements of the joint, while the coracoclavicular ligaments give most of the vertical stability.
