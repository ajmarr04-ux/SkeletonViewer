The posterior sternoclavicular ligament is a band covering the back of the sternoclavicular joint. It runs from the back of the sternal end of the clavicle to the back of the manubrium of the sternum. It reinforces the capsule behind the joint and stops the medial end of the clavicle moving backwards.
