The fifth lumbar vertebra has the largest body of all the vertebrae, deeper at the front than at the back, which forms the lumbosacral angle. Its thick transverse processes are anchored to the iliac crests by the iliolumbar ligaments, and it rests on the sacrum at the lumbosacral joint.
