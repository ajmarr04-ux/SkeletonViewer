The metatarsal interosseous ligaments are strong short bands that pass between the facing sides of the bases of neighbouring metatarsal bones. They lie deep within the intermetatarsal joints and unite the second to fifth metatarsals firmly at their bases. They resist spreading of the metatarsals and keep the forefoot stable.
