The dorsal cuboideonavicular ligament is a short transverse band on the upper surface of the midfoot. It runs from the dorsal surface of the cuboid to the dorsal surface of the navicular bone. It ties the two bones together where they meet, usually by a fibrous joint, and so adds to the stability of the midfoot.
