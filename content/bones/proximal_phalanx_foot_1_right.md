The proximal phalanx of the big toe is the larger of its two phalanges; the big toe has no middle phalanx. Its concave base articulates with the head of the first metatarsal, and its head with the distal phalanx. The short muscles of the big toe, with the sesamoid bones, insert around its base and steady the toe as the body rolls forward over it.
