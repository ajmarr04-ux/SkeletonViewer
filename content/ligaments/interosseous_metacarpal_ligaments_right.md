The interosseous metacarpal ligaments are short, strong fibres that join the adjoining surfaces of the bases of the second to fifth metacarpals, in the gaps between the articular facets. They bind neighbouring bases firmly together and limit separation, so the metacarpal bases act as a stable row while the heads remain freer.
