Extensor indicis is a narrow muscle arising from the back of the ulna, below extensor pollicis longus, and from the interosseous membrane. Its tendon passes with those of extensor digitorum in the fourth dorsal compartment and joins the ulnar side of the index finger's extensor tendon. It lets the index finger be extended on its own, as in pointing, and helps extend the wrist.
