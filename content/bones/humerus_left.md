The humerus is the long bone of the upper arm. Its rounded head fits the glenoid cavity of the scapula at the shoulder. The greater and lesser tubercles anchor the rotator cuff, and the deltoid tuberosity marks the middle of the shaft. At the elbow, the capitulum meets the radius and the trochlea meets the ulna, between the two epicondyles.
