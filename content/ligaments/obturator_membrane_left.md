The obturator membrane is a thin fibrous sheet that almost completely closes the obturator foramen of the hip bone. Its edges attach to the margin of the foramen, except above, where a gap, the obturator canal, lets the obturator nerve and vessels pass into the thigh. Obturator internus and externus muscles arise from its two surfaces.
