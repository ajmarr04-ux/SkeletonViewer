Thyro-arytenoid is a broad, thin sheet lateral to the vocal fold. It arises from the inner surface of the thyroid cartilage near the angle and runs backwards to the arytenoid cartilage. Its innermost fibres, beside the vocal ligament, form vocalis. It draws the arytenoids forwards, relaxing and shortening the vocal folds and lowering pitch, and helps close the glottis.
