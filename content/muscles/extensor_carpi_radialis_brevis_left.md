Extensor carpi radialis brevis is a shorter muscle lying under extensor carpi radialis longus. It arises from the lateral epicondyle by the common extensor tendon and inserts on the back of the base of the third metacarpal, sharing the second dorsal compartment at the wrist. It extends the wrist and helps abduct it, and holds the wrist steady during a firm grip.
