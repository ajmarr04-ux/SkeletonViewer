The radiate ligament is a fan-shaped band on the front of the joint of the head of a rib. Its fibres spread from the front of the head to the sides of the bodies of the two adjoining vertebrae and the intervertebral disc between them. It stabilises the rib head against the vertebral column and reinforces the front of the joint capsule.
