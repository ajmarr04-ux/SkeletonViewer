The transverse ligament of the knee is a short band of fibres that crosses the front of the tibial plateau. It joins the anterior convex margin of the lateral meniscus to the anterior horn of the medial meniscus. It ties the front ends of the two menisci together, helping them to move in step as the knee flexes and extends. It is not always present.
