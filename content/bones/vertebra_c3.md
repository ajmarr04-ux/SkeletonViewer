The third cervical vertebra is a typical cervical vertebra: a small, broad body with raised lateral lips (uncinate processes), a large triangular vertebral foramen, transverse processes pierced by foramina for the vertebral artery, and a short bifid spinous process. It joins C2 above and C4 below at the disc and facet joints.
