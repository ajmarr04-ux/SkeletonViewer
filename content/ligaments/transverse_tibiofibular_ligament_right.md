The transverse tibiofibular ligament is a strong, thick band lying below the posterior tibiofibular ligament, and is often described as its deep part. It runs from the lateral malleolus across the back of the ankle to the posterior border of the tibia. It forms part of the articular socket for the talus and helps to hold the tibia and fibula together.
