The costoclavicular ligament is a short, strong band that links the underside of the sternal end of the clavicle to the upper surface of the first rib and its costal cartilage. It is formed by anterior and posterior laminae. It helps to stabilise the sternoclavicular joint and limits elevation of the clavicle.
