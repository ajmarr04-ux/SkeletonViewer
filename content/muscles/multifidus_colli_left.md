Multifidus colli (multifidus cervicis) is the cervical part of multifidus. Its fascicles arise from the articular processes of the lower four cervical vertebrae and pass upwards and medially to the spinous processes of C2 to C5, deep to semispinalis colli. It stabilises the cervical segments and helps extend the neck and turn it to the opposite side.
