The palmar metacarpal ligaments are transverse bands on the palm side of the hand that connect the palmar surfaces of the bases of neighbouring metacarpals, from the second to the fifth. They reinforce the intermetacarpal joints from the front and help to keep the bases of the metacarpals together.
