The ulnotriquetral ligament is a palmar ligament on the ulnar side of the wrist. It runs from the triangular fibrocartilage complex, at the distal end of the ulna, to the palmar surface of the triquetrum. It belongs to the palmar ulnocarpal complex, supports the joint capsule and ties the triquetrum to the ulnar side of the forearm.
