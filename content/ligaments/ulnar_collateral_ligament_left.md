The ulnar collateral ligament is a triangular band on the medial side of the elbow. Its apex attaches to the medial epicondyle of the humerus, and it fans out into anterior and posterior bands that reach the coronoid process and olecranon of the ulna, with a transverse band joining them. It is the main restraint against valgus force at the elbow.
