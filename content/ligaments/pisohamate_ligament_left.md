The pisohamate ligament is a strong band that runs from the pisiform to the hook of the hamate. It is in effect a continuation of the tendon of flexor carpi ulnaris, which is attached to the pisiform. It holds the pisiform in place and transmits the pull of the muscle onward to the hamate, and so to the fifth metacarpal.
