Abductor pollicis longus lies just below supinator on the back of the forearm, arising from the ulna, the radius and the interosseous membrane between them. Its tendon winds round the lateral side of the radius with extensor pollicis brevis, forming the front border of the anatomical snuffbox, to the base of the first metacarpal. It abducts and extends the thumb at the carpometacarpal joint and helps abduct the wrist.
