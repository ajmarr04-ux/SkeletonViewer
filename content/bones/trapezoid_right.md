The trapezoid is the smallest bone of the distal carpal row, shaped like a wedge with its broad end at the back of the hand. It articulates with the scaphoid above, the trapezium and capitate at its sides, and the base of the second metacarpal below. It is firmly held by ligaments and is rarely injured on its own.
