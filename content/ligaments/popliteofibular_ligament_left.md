The popliteofibular ligament is a small but important band of the posterolateral corner of the knee. It runs from the tendon of popliteus, near its musculotendinous junction, downwards and laterally to the back of the head of the fibula. It ties the popliteus tendon to the fibula and helps to stabilise the lateral side of the knee against posterolateral rotation.
