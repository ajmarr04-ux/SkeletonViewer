The radius is the lateral bone of the forearm, on the thumb side. Its small disc-shaped head turns against the capitulum of the humerus and the radial notch of the ulna. The shaft widens towards the wrist, where the broad distal end carries the scaphoid and lunate. Turning the palm over swings the radius around the ulna.
