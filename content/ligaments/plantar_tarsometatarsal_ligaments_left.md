The plantar tarsometatarsal ligaments are bands on the sole of the midfoot. They run from the plantar surfaces of the cuneiforms and the cuboid to the plantar surfaces of the bases of the metatarsals. They are less regular than the dorsal ligaments, but they strengthen the underside of the tarsometatarsal joints and help to support the arches.
