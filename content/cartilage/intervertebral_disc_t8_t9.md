The T8-T9 intervertebral disc lies between the bodies of two adjacent thoracic vertebrae, joining them. A soft gelatinous nucleus pulposus is enclosed by fibrous rings, the anulus fibrosus. Thoracic discs are thin compared with those above and below, and the ribs and sternum limit movement here, so the thoracic spine is the stiffest part of the column.
