The third metatarsal lies in the middle of the forefoot. Its base articulates with the lateral cuneiform behind and with the second and fourth metatarsals at its sides. Its head carries the proximal phalanx of the third toe. Like the second metatarsal, it moves very little and is a common site of stress fractures.
