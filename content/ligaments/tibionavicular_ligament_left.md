The tibionavicular ligament is the most anterior part of the superficial layer of the medial (deltoid) ligament of the ankle. It runs forwards from the medial malleolus of the tibia to the dorsomedial surface of the navicular bone, and blends with the plantar calcaneonavicular ligament. It helps to restrain eversion of the foot and supports the medial side of the ankle.
