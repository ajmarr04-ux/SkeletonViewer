The ethmoid is a light, delicate bone between the orbits. Its cribriform plate forms the roof of the nasal cavity and lets the olfactory nerves through; its perpendicular plate forms the upper nasal septum; and its two labyrinths contain the ethmoidal air cells and carry the superior and middle nasal conchae.
