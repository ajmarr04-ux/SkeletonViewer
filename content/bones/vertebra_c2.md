The axis is the second cervical vertebra. Its distinctive feature is the dens, a tooth-like peg rising from its body, around which the atlas turns, held in place by the transverse ligament. Its large, bifid spinous process is the first one easily felt below the skull and anchors muscles that rotate the head.
