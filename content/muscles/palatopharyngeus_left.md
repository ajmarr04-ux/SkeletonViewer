Palatopharyngeus is a thin, longitudinal muscle that arises from the hard palate and the palatine aponeurosis of the soft palate. It forms the palatopharyngeal arch behind the palatine tonsil and descends inside the pharyngeal wall, joining stylopharyngeus to reach the thyroid cartilage. In swallowing it raises the pharynx and larynx, narrows the oropharyngeal isthmus and draws the palate downwards.
