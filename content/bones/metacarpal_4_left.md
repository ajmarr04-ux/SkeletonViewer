The fourth metacarpal, in the ring finger, is thinner and shorter than the third. Its small base articulates with the hamate and capitate and with the third and fifth metacarpals. It has a little more movement than the second and third, which helps the palm cup around an object. Its head forms the knuckle of the ring finger.
