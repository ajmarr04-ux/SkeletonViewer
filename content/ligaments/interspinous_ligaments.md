The interspinous ligaments are thin membranous bands that fill the space between adjacent spinous processes, each running from the root to the apex of one process to the next. They are best developed in the lumbar region and are weak in the neck, where the nuchal ligament takes over. They limit flexion and are continuous with the supraspinous ligament behind.
