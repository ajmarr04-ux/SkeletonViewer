Coccygeus (ischiococcygeus) is a triangular sheet of muscle and fibrous tissue at the back of the pelvic floor, behind levator ani. It arises from the ischial spine and fans out to the lateral margins of the lower sacrum and the coccyx, lying on the deep surface of the sacrospinous ligament, with which it is partly fused. It supports the pelvic viscera and pulls the coccyx forwards after defecation and childbirth.
