The internal intercostal muscles form the middle layer of each intercostal space. Their fibres run downwards and backwards, at right angles to the external intercostals, from the floor of the costal groove of one rib to the upper border of the rib below, from the sternum in front to the angles of the ribs behind. Their interosseous parts lower the ribs in forced expiration.
