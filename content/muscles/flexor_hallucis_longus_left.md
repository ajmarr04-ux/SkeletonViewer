Flexor hallucis longus is the strongest of the deep calf muscles, arising from the lower two thirds of the back of the fibula. Its tendon grooves the back of the talus, passes under the sustentaculum tali and runs along the sole to the base of the distal phalanx of the big toe. It flexes the big toe, giving the final push-off in walking.
