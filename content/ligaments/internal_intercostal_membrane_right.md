The internal intercostal membrane is a thin fibrous sheet in the intercostal spaces between the angles of the ribs and the vertebral column, where the internal intercostal muscle is absent. Its fibres run obliquely downwards and medially, in line with the muscle, between adjacent ribs. It closes the back of each space and lies deep to the erector spinae.
