The posterior ligament of the fibular head is a single thick band. It runs obliquely upwards and medially from the back of the head of the fibula to the back of the lateral tibial condyle. It is covered by the tendon of popliteus and reinforces the back of the capsule of the proximal tibiofibular joint, binding the fibula to the tibia.
