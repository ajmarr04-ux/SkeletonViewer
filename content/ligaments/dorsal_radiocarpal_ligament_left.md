The dorsal radiocarpal ligament is a broad band on the back of the wrist. It runs obliquely from the posterior margin of the distal radius, downwards and medially, to the dorsal surfaces of the scaphoid, lunate and triquetrum. It reinforces the back of the joint capsule, keeps the proximal carpal row moving with the radius, and checks excessive flexion of the wrist.
