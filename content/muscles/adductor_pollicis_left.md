Adductor pollicis is a fan-shaped muscle deep in the palm, between the thumb and the palm. Its oblique head arises from the capitate and the bases of the second and third metacarpals, and its transverse head from the shaft of the third metacarpal; they converge on the medial side of the base of the proximal phalanx of the thumb. It pulls the thumb towards the palm, giving power to grip.
