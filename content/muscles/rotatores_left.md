The rotatores are the deepest and shortest of the transversospinal muscles, best developed in the thoracic region. Each small quadrilateral slip arises from the root of a transverse process and passes to the lamina and root of the spinous process of the vertebra one level (short rotatores) or two levels (long rotatores) above. They act mainly as stabilisers and position sensors of the vertebrae.
