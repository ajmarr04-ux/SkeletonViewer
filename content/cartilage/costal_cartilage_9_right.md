The ninth costal cartilage continues the front end of the ninth rib, a false rib. It does not reach the sternum but turns upwards to join the lower border of the eighth costal cartilage, helping to form the costal margin of the thoracic cage. Its elasticity lets the lower chest wall move during breathing.
