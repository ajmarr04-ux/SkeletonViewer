Obturator externus is a flat, triangular muscle covering the outer surface of the obturator membrane and the bone around it. Its fibres converge on a tendon that passes behind the neck of the femur to the trochanteric fossa. It laterally rotates the hip and helps hold the head of the femur in the acetabulum.
