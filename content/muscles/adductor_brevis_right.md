Adductor brevis lies behind pectineus and adductor longus. It arises from the body and inferior ramus of the pubis and widens as it descends to insert on the pectineal line and the upper part of the linea aspera. The two branches of the obturator nerve pass in front of and behind it. It adducts the thigh and helps flex the hip.
