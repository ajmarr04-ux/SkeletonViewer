Abductor pollicis brevis is the most superficial thenar muscle, forming the lateral part of the ball of the thumb. It arises from the flexor retinaculum and the tubercles of the scaphoid and trapezium, and inserts on the lateral side of the base of the proximal phalanx of the thumb. It abducts the thumb, drawing it forward away from the palm at a right angle, and helps in opposition.
