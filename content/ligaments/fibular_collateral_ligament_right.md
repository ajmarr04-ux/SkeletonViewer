The fibular collateral ligament is a rounded cord on the lateral side of the knee. It runs from the lateral epicondyle of the femur downwards and slightly backwards to the head of the fibula, where it is split by the tendon of biceps femoris. It is separate from the joint capsule and the lateral meniscus, and it resists forces that push the knee into varus.
