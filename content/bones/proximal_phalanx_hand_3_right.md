The proximal phalanx of the middle finger is the longest of the proximal phalanges. Its base articulates with the head of the third metacarpal at the knuckle, and its pulley-shaped head with the middle phalanx at the proximal interphalangeal joint. Two dorsal interossei insert on either side of its base, so it can be moved towards either neighbour.
