The triquetrum is a pyramid-shaped bone at the little-finger end of the proximal carpal row. It articulates with the lunate beside it and the hamate below, and carries the pisiform on an oval facet on its front. Its upper surface sits against the articular disc of the distal radio-ulnar joint rather than the ulna itself.
