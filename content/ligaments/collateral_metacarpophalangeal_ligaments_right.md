The collateral ligaments of the metacarpophalangeal joints are strong cords on each side of every knuckle joint. Each runs obliquely forwards and downwards from the side of the metacarpal head to the base of the proximal phalanx. They are slack in extension, allowing spreading of the fingers, and tighten when the joint is flexed.
