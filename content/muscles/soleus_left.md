Soleus is the broad, flat muscle beneath gastrocnemius, named for its sole-fish shape. It arises from the head and upper back of the fibula, the soleal line of the tibia and a tendinous arch between them, and joins the calcaneal tendon. It plantarflexes the ankle and, rich in fatigue-resistant fibres, steadies the leg over the foot in standing.
