The quadrate ligament is a thin fibrous band that passes from the lower margin of the radial notch of the ulna to the neck of the radius. It lies just below the annular ligament and braces the lower part of the capsule of the proximal radio-ulnar joint. It helps to hold the radial head in place during rotation.
