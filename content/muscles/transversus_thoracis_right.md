Transversus thoracis is a thin sheet on the inner surface of the front of the chest wall, behind the sternum and the internal thoracic vessels. It arises from the back of the lower sternum and the xiphoid process, and its slips fan out upwards and laterally to the inner surfaces of the second to sixth costal cartilages. It weakly pulls the costal cartilages down in expiration.
