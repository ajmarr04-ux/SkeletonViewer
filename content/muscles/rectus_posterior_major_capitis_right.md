Rectus capitis posterior major arises by a pointed tendon from the spinous process of the axis and widens as it runs up and laterally to the lateral part of the inferior nuchal line of the occipital bone. It forms the medial side of the suboccipital triangle. It extends the head and turns the face to its own side.
