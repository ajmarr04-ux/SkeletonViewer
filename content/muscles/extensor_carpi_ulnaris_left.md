Extensor carpi ulnaris is the most medial superficial extensor of the forearm. Its humeral and ulnar heads join in a tendon that runs in the groove beside the ulnar styloid process, in the sixth dorsal compartment, to the tubercle on the base of the fifth metacarpal. It extends and adducts (ulnar deviates) the wrist, and with flexor carpi ulnaris it is the main ulnar deviator.
