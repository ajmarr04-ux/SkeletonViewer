The palmar interossei are three small muscles on the palmar side of the metacarpals, each arising from one bone: the second, fourth and fifth. They insert on the proximal phalanx and extensor expansion of the same finger, on the side facing the middle finger. They adduct the index, ring and little fingers towards the middle finger, and help the lumbricals flex the knuckles and extend the finger joints.
