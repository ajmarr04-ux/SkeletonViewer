The scaphocapitate ligament is a short palmar intercarpal ligament joining the scaphoid to the capitate across the midcarpal joint. It is one of the ligaments that tie the proximal and distal rows of carpal bones together on the front of the wrist, helping the two rows move as a linked unit and limiting separation of the scaphoid from the capitate.
