Gluteus maximus is the large, coarse-fibred muscle that shapes the buttock. It arises from the back of the ilium, the sacrum, the coccyx and the sacrotuberous ligament, and most of it inserts into the iliotibial tract, the rest on the gluteal tuberosity of the femur. It extends and laterally rotates the hip, powerfully in climbing, rising from a chair and running.
