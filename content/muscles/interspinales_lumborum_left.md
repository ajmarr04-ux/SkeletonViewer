The interspinales lumborum are paired bundles joining the spinous processes of adjacent lumbar vertebrae, from L1 down to the sacrum, one on each side of the interspinous ligament. They help extend the lumbar spine and, rich in muscle spindles, steady the vertebrae and monitor their position.
