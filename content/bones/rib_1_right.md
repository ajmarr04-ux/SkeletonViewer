The first rib is the shortest, broadest and most curved rib, lying almost flat at the top of the thoracic cage. Its head articulates with T1 only. On its upper surface the scalene tubercle separates grooves for the subclavian vein in front and the subclavian artery behind. A fracture of this rib suggests a high-energy injury.
