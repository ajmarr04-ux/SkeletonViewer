The triquetrocapitate ligament is a palmar intercarpal ligament running from the triquetrum to the capitate across the midcarpal joint. Together with the scaphocapitate ligament it forms part of the arcuate (deltoid) arrangement of ligaments on the front of the wrist, and it helps to hold the proximal and distal carpal rows together.
