The collateral metatarsophalangeal ligaments are strong cords on the medial and lateral sides of each metatarsophalangeal joint. Each runs obliquely forwards and downwards from the tubercle on the side of the metatarsal head to the base of the proximal phalanx and the plantar plate. They reinforce the joint capsules and limit sideways movement of the toes.
