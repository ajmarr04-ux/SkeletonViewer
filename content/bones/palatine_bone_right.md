The palatine bone is an L-shaped bone at the back of the nasal cavity. Its horizontal plate forms the back of the hard palate, meeting its partner in the midline, and its perpendicular plate forms part of the side wall of the nasal cavity, reaching up to the floor of the orbit. Nerves to the palate pass through its foramina.
