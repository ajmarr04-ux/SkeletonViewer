Adductor longus is the most anterior of the adductors, a large fan-shaped muscle whose rounded tendon is easily felt in the groin. It arises from the body of the pubis below the pubic crest and spreads out to insert on the middle third of the linea aspera. It adducts the thigh and helps flex the hip.
