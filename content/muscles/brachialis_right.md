Brachialis is a broad, flat muscle lying deep to biceps in the lower half of the arm. It arises from the front of the distal humerus and inserts on the coronoid process and tuberosity of the ulna. Because it pulls on the ulna, which does not rotate, it flexes the elbow equally in every position of the forearm and is the main flexor of the elbow.
