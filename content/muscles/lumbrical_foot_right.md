The four lumbricals of the foot are small muscles arising from the tendons of flexor digitorum longus in the sole. Each passes along the medial side of a toe to the extensor expansion over its proximal phalanx. They flex the metatarsophalangeal joints and extend the interphalangeal joints of the second to fifth toes, keeping the toes from buckling as the heel lifts.
