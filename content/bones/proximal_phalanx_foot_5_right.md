The proximal phalanx of the little toe is the shortest of the proximal phalanges of the foot. Its base articulates with the head of the fifth metatarsal and its head with the middle phalanx. Abductor digiti minimi and flexor digiti minimi brevis insert on the outer side of its base and move the little toe.
