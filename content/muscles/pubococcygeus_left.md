Pubococcygeus is the main part of levator ani. It arises from the back of the body of the pubis and the front of the tendinous arch over obturator internus and runs backwards beside the urethra, vagina in women and anal canal to the anococcygeal body and the coccyx. With the other parts of levator ani it forms the muscular floor of the pelvis.
