Opponens digiti minimi is the deepest hypothenar muscle, lying under the abductor and flexor. It arises from the hook of the hamate and the flexor retinaculum and inserts along the medial border of the fifth metacarpal. It draws the fifth metacarpal forwards and rotates it at the carpometacarpal joint, deepening the hollow of the palm and bringing the little finger to meet the thumb.
