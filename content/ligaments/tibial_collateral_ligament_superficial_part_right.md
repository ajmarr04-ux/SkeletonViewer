The superficial part of the tibial collateral ligament is a long, flat band on the medial side of the knee. It runs from the medial epicondyle of the femur downwards to the medial surface of the tibia, behind the attachment of the pes anserinus and below the level of the tibial tuberosity. It resists valgus forces and is the main medial stabiliser of the joint.
