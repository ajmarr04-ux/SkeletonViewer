The interspinales colli (interspinales cervicis) are short paired bundles, one on each side of the interspinous ligament, joining the bifid spinous processes of adjacent cervical vertebrae from C2 to T1. They are well developed in the neck. They help extend the cervical spine and, being rich in muscle spindles, steady the vertebrae and sense their position.
