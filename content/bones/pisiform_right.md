The pisiform is a small, pea-shaped bone on the front of the triquetrum, easy to feel at the base of the hypothenar eminence. It develops within the tendon of flexor carpi ulnaris, so it is a sesamoid bone, and it articulates only with the triquetrum. Ligaments carry the pull of the tendon on to the hamate and the fifth metacarpal.
