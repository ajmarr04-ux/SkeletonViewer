The first costal cartilage is short, thick and stout, running from the lower border of the first rib's front end down and medially to the manubrium of the sternum. It joins the manubrium directly as a primary cartilaginous joint that allows almost no movement, and it also lies beneath the clavicle. Its firmness helps keep the top of the thoracic cage rigid.
