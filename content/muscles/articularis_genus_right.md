Articularis genus is a small, flat muscle of a few slips beneath vastus intermedius, often blended with it. It arises from the front of the lower femoral shaft and inserts into the suprapatellar bursa and the synovial membrane of the knee. It pulls the bursa up as the knee extends, so the membrane is not pinched between the patella and femur.
