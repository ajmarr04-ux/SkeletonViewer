The fifth thoracic vertebra is a typical thoracic vertebra, with a heart-shaped body, a small round vertebral foramen and a long spinous process sloping down over the vertebra below. Demifacets on its body take the heads of ribs 5 and 6, and its transverse costal facets take the tubercles of rib 5.
