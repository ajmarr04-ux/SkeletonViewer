Platysma is a broad, thin sheet of muscle in the superficial fascia of the neck, belonging developmentally to the muscles of facial expression. It arises from the fascia over the upper chest and shoulder, crosses the clavicle and runs up the neck to the lower border of the mandible and the skin of the lower face. It tenses the skin of the neck and draws the mouth downwards.
