Medial pterygoid is a thick quadrilateral muscle on the inner side of the ramus, mirroring masseter on the outer side so that the two form a sling around the angle of the mandible. Its deep head arises from the medial surface of the lateral pterygoid plate and the palatine bone, its small superficial head from the maxillary tuberosity. It closes the jaw, helps protrude it and grinds from side to side.
