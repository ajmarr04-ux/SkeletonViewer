The interpubic disc is a pad of fibrocartilage lying between the two pubic bodies at the midline, in the pubic symphysis. Hyaline cartilage covers the facing bony surfaces, and ligaments strengthen the joint above and below. It binds the halves of the pelvis together while allowing a very small amount of movement.
