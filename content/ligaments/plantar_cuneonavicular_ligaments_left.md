The plantar cuneonavicular ligaments are a set of bands on the sole of the foot. They run from the plantar surface of the navicular to the plantar surfaces of the three cuneiform bones. They bridge the cuneonavicular joints, holding the navicular to the cuneiforms and strengthening the underside of the medial part of the tarsus.
