The lower central incisor is the smallest permanent tooth. Its narrow, chisel-shaped crown meets the inner surfaces of the upper incisors, and its single root, flattened from side to side, sits in the alveolar part of the mandible. It is usually the first permanent tooth to erupt, at about six to seven years.
