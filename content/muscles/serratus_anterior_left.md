Serratus anterior is a broad sheet over the side of the chest, named for its saw-toothed digitations. It arises from the outer surfaces of the upper eight or nine ribs and passes back around the chest wall to the costal surface of the medial border of the scapula. It holds the scapula against the chest, pulls it forwards in punching and pushing, and rotates it upwards to raise the arm overhead.
