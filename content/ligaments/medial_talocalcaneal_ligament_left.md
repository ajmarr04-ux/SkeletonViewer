The medial talocalcaneal ligament is a short band on the medial side of the subtalar joint. It runs from the medial tubercle of the posterior process of the talus to the back of the sustentaculum tali of the calcaneus. It reinforces the capsule on the inner side of the heel and helps to keep the talus and calcaneus aligned.
