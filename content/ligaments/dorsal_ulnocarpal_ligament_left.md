The dorsal ulnocarpal ligament is a band on the back of the ulnar side of the wrist. It passes from the distal ulna and the triangular fibrocartilage complex across the back of the wrist to the triquetrum. It reinforces the dorsal joint capsule on the ulnar side and helps to link the forearm to the carpus.
