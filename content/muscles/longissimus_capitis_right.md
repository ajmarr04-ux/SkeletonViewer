Longissimus capitis is a slender band lying deep to splenius capitis. It arises from the transverse processes of the upper four or five thoracic vertebrae and the articular processes of the lower four cervical vertebrae, and ascends to the back of the mastoid process. It extends the head and, on one side, tilts the head and turns the face to the same side.
