The collateral interphalangeal ligaments of the foot are strong cords on the medial and lateral sides of each interphalangeal joint of the toes. Each runs from the head of the more proximal phalanx forwards and downwards to the base of the next phalanx. They reinforce the joint capsules and limit sideways movement while allowing the toes to flex and extend.
