The acetabular labrum is a ring of fibrocartilage fixed to the rim of the acetabulum of the hip bone. Across the acetabular notch it is bridged by the transverse acetabular ligament. It deepens the socket, grips the femoral head slightly more than half way round, and helps seal the joint.
