The ulna is the medial bone of the forearm, on the little-finger side. Its upper end is hook-shaped: the olecranon and the coronoid process grip the trochlea of the humerus to form the elbow hinge, and the olecranon is the point of the elbow. Its small distal head sits against the radius, separated from the carpus by an articular disc.
