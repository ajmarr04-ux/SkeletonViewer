The vomer is a thin, flat, ploughshare-shaped bone in the midline of the nasal cavity. It forms the lower and back part of the nasal septum, joined above to the perpendicular plate of the ethmoid and in front to the septal cartilage. Its upper border spreads into two alae that rest on the body of the sphenoid.
