Splenius capitis is a broad, strap-like muscle that bandages the deeper muscles of the back of the neck. It arises from the lower half of the nuchal ligament and the spinous processes of C7 to T3, and runs upwards and laterally to the mastoid process and the lateral third of the superior nuchal line, deep to sternocleidomastoid. It extends the head and neck and turns the face to its own side.
