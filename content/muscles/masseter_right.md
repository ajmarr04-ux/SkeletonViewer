Masseter is a thick, quadrilateral muscle covering the lateral surface of the ramus of the mandible, palpable at the angle of the jaw when the teeth are clenched. It runs from the zygomatic arch to the ramus and angle in superficial and deep parts. It is a powerful elevator of the mandible in biting and chewing, and helps move the jaw from side to side.
