The deep transverse metatarsal ligament is a set of short, broad bands that run across the sole of the forefoot between the plantar plates of the metatarsophalangeal joints. It connects the heads of the metatarsals. It limits spreading of the metatarsal heads and helps to hold the transverse arch of the forefoot.
