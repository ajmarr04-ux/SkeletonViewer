Flexor pollicis longus lies beside flexor digitorum profundus on the front of the radius and the interosseous membrane. Its tendon passes through the carpal tunnel in its own sheath and between the sesamoids of the thumb to the base of the distal phalanx. It is the only muscle that flexes the interphalangeal joint of the thumb, and it helps flex the joints proximal to it.
