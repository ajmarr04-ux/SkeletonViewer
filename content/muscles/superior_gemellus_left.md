Superior gemellus is a small, narrow muscle arising from the outer surface of the ischial spine. It runs laterally along the upper border of the obturator internus tendon and blends with it, inserting with that tendon on the medial surface of the greater trochanter. It helps rotate the extended hip laterally and steadies the head of the femur.
