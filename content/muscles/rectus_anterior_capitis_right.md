Rectus capitis anterior is a short, flat muscle behind longus capitis. It arises from the front of the lateral mass and the root of the transverse process of the atlas and passes up and medially to the inferior surface of the basilar part of the occipital bone, just in front of the occipital condyle. It flexes the head at the atlanto-occipital joints.
