Internal oblique is the intermediate flat muscle of the abdominal wall. It arises from the thoracolumbar fascia, the anterior two-thirds of the iliac crest and the lateral part of the inguinal ligament; its fibres fan upwards and medially to the lower three ribs and an aponeurosis that splits around rectus abdominis to the linea alba. Its lowest fibres join transversus in the conjoint tendon.
