The ligament of the head of the femur is a flat, triangular band inside the hip joint, covered by synovial membrane. Its narrow end attaches to the fovea of the femoral head, and its broad end splits to attach to either side of the acetabular notch and to the transverse acetabular ligament. It is slack in most positions and gives little mechanical support.
