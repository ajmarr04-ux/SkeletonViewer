The distal phalanx of the little finger is the smallest bone of the hand's fingers. Its base articulates with the middle phalanx at the distal interphalangeal joint, and its tip widens into a tuberosity under the nail. Flexor digitorum profundus inserts on its palm surface, and the extensor tendon on the back of its base.
