The calcaneofibular ligament is a rounded cord of the lateral collateral ligament of the ankle. It passes downwards and slightly backwards from the tip of the lateral malleolus to a small tubercle on the lateral surface of the calcaneus, deep to the fibular tendons. It crosses both the ankle and subtalar joints and resists inversion of the heel.
