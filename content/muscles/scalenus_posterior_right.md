Scalenus posterior is the smallest and deepest of the scalenes, often blended with scalenus medius. It arises from the posterior tubercles of the transverse processes of C4 to C6 and passes down behind scalenus medius to the outer surface of the second rib. It raises the second rib in forced inspiration and bends the neck to its own side.
