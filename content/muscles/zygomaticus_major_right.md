Zygomaticus major is the principal muscle of smiling. It arises from the lateral surface of the zygomatic bone in front of the zygomaticotemporal suture and runs obliquely downwards and medially across the cheek to the modiolus at the angle of the mouth, blending with levator anguli oris and orbicularis oris. It draws the corner of the mouth upwards and laterally.
