Digastric has two bellies joined by an intermediate tendon that is held to the hyoid by a fibrous sling. The posterior belly runs down and forwards from the mastoid notch; the anterior belly runs back from the digastric fossa of the mandible. Together they raise the hyoid and steady it in swallowing and speech, and, with the hyoid fixed, help open the mouth.
