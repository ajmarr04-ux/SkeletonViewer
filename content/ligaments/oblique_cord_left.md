The oblique cord is a small, weak band of fibrous tissue lying on the deep surface of supinator, near the elbow. It runs from the lateral side of the ulnar tuberosity of the ulna downwards and laterally to the radius just below its tuberosity. It adds a little to the connection between the two forearm bones.
