Multifidus thoracis is the thinner thoracic part of multifidus, lying deep to semispinalis and longissimus. Its fascicles arise from the transverse processes of the thoracic vertebrae and pass upwards and medially over two to four segments to the spinous processes of the vertebrae above, as high as C6. It steadies the thoracic vertebrae and helps extend and rotate the spine.
