Vastus lateralis is the largest part of the quadriceps, forming the bulk of the outer thigh. It arises by a broad aponeurosis from the greater trochanter, the intertrochanteric line, the gluteal tuberosity and the lateral lip of the linea aspera. It inserts on the lateral side of the patella and quadriceps tendon, reaching the tibia through the patellar ligament, and extends the knee.
