Temporalis is a broad, fan-shaped muscle that fills the temporal fossa. It arises from the floor of the fossa, below the inferior temporal line, and from the deep surface of the temporal fascia. Its fibres converge on a tendon that passes deep to the zygomatic arch to the coronoid process and anterior border of the ramus. It closes the jaw, and its posterior, nearly horizontal fibres retract the mandible.
