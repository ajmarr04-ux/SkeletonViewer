Stylohyoid is a slender muscle running alongside the posterior belly of digastric. It arises from the back of the styloid process and passes down and forwards to the junction of the body and greater horn of the hyoid, where it splits around the intermediate tendon of digastric. It draws the hyoid upwards and backwards, elongating the floor of the mouth during swallowing.
