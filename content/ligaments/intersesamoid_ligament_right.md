The intersesamoid ligament is a small band beneath the head of the first metatarsal, at the base of the big toe. It joins the medial and lateral sesamoid bones embedded in the plantar plate of the first metatarsophalangeal joint. It keeps the two sesamoids together as they glide on the plantar surface of the metatarsal head.
