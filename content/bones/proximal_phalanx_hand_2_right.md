The proximal phalanx of the index finger is the longest bone of that finger. Its concave base articulates with the head of the second metacarpal at the knuckle, and its pulley-shaped head with the middle phalanx. Interosseous muscles insert at its base and, with the lumbricals through the extensor expansion, help steady the finger for pinch and precise grip.
