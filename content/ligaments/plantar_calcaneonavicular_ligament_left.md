The plantar calcaneonavicular ligament, often called the spring ligament, is a broad, thick band on the sole of the foot. It runs from the front of the sustentaculum tali of the calcaneus to the plantar surface of the navicular. Its upper surface is covered with cartilage and forms part of the socket that supports the head of the talus, so it helps to maintain the medial longitudinal arch.
