The ulnolunate ligament is a palmar ligament on the ulnar side of the wrist. It runs from the triangular fibrocartilage complex, at the distal end of the ulna, to the palmar surface of the lunate. It forms part of the palmar ulnocarpal complex, supports the front of the joint capsule and ties the lunate to the ulnar side of the forearm.
