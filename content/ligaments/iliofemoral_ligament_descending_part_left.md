The descending part of the iliofemoral ligament, also called the medial or vertical band, is the lower limb of the Y-shaped ligament at the front of the hip. It runs from the lower part of the anterior inferior iliac spine and acetabular rim down to the lower end of the intertrochanteric line of the femur. It becomes taut in extension and limits that movement.
