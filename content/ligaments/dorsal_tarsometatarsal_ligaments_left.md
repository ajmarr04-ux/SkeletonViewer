The dorsal tarsometatarsal ligaments are flat bands on the upper surface of the midfoot. They run from the dorsal surfaces of the medial, intermediate and lateral cuneiforms and the cuboid to the dorsal surfaces of the bases of the five metatarsals. They strengthen the tarsometatarsal joints and hold the metatarsal bases to the tarsus.
