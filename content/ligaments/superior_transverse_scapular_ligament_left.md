The superior transverse scapular ligament is a thin, flat band that crosses the suprascapular notch on the upper border of the scapula. It runs from the base of the coracoid process to the medial side of the notch. The suprascapular nerve passes under the ligament, while the suprascapular artery usually passes above it.
