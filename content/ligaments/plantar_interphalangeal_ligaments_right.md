The plantar interphalangeal ligaments are thick fibrocartilaginous plates in the floor of each interphalangeal joint of the toes. Each is attached firmly to the base of the more distal phalanx and loosely to the neck of the phalanx behind. They strengthen the underside of the joint capsule, resist overextension and give a smooth surface for the flexor tendons.
