The four dorsal interossei of the foot fill the spaces between the metatarsals, each arising by two heads from the adjacent bones. The first inserts on the medial side of the second toe, and the second to fourth on the lateral sides of the second to fourth toes. They abduct the toes from the line of the second toe and help flex the metatarsophalangeal joints.
