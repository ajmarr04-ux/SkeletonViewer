The fourth thoracic vertebra is a typical thoracic vertebra, with a heart-shaped body and a long spinous process that overlaps the vertebra below. Demifacets on its body take the heads of ribs 4 and 5, and its transverse costal facets take the tubercles of rib 4. The arch of the aorta ends beside it.
