The lateral cuneiform is the middle-sized cuneiform, wedged between the intermediate cuneiform and the cuboid. Behind, it articulates with the navicular; in front, mainly with the third metatarsal, and also with the second and fourth. Its wedge shape, with the narrow edge towards the sole, helps form the transverse arch of the foot.
