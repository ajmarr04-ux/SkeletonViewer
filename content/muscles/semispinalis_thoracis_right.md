Semispinalis thoracis is the lowest part of semispinalis, the most superficial layer of the transversospinal muscles. It consists of thin bundles with long tendons that arise from the transverse processes of T6 to T10 and ascend, spanning four to six segments, to the spinous processes of C6 to T4. It extends the thoracic spine and turns it to the opposite side.
