Iliococcygeus is the thin posterolateral part of levator ani. It arises from the ischial spine and the back of the tendinous arch over obturator internus and passes backwards and medially to the anococcygeal body and the sides of the coccyx. It forms much of the pelvic diaphragm behind, supporting the pelvic viscera.
