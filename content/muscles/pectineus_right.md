Pectineus is a flat, quadrangular muscle at the top of the medial thigh, forming part of the floor of the femoral triangle. It arises from the pecten pubis on the superior pubic ramus and runs down and back to the pectineal line of the femur, just below the lesser trochanter. It adducts and flexes the hip.
