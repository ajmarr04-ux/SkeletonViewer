The middle phalanx of the ring finger links the proximal and distal phalanges by two hinge joints. Its base fits the head of the proximal phalanx, and its head the distal phalanx. Flexor digitorum superficialis inserts on its palm surface and the central slip of the extensor tendon on the back of its base.
