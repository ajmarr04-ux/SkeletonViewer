The second costal cartilage is a short bar of hyaline cartilage continuing the front end of the second rib. It passes medially to meet the sternum at the junction of the manubrium and body, the sternal angle, where it forms a small synovial sternocostal joint. It lets the rib move slightly and gives the front of the chest wall its elasticity.
