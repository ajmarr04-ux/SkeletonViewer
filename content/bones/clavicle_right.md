The clavicle is an S-shaped long bone lying horizontally at the root of the neck. Its rounded sternal end articulates with the manubrium of the sternum, and its flattened acromial end with the acromion of the scapula. It holds the shoulder away from the trunk so the arm can swing freely, and it lies just under the skin along its whole length.
