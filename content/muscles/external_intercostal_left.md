The external intercostal muscles are the outermost of the three layers in each intercostal space. Each runs from the lower border of one rib downwards and forwards to the upper border of the rib below, from the tubercles behind to the costochondral junctions in front, where the external intercostal membrane replaces it. They raise the ribs and are most active in inspiration.
