The trapezium is the bone at the thumb end of the distal carpal row. Its saddle-shaped lower surface carries the base of the first metacarpal, making the carpometacarpal joint that gives the thumb its wide range of movement. It also articulates with the scaphoid, the trapezoid and the second metacarpal. A tubercle and groove on its front guide the flexor carpi radialis tendon.
