The C2-C3 intervertebral disc lies between the bodies of two adjacent cervical vertebrae, joining them. Its soft gelatinous centre, the nucleus pulposus, is held by concentric rings of tough fibrous tissue, the anulus fibrosus. Cervical discs are thicker in front than behind, which helps to maintain the forward curve of the neck, and they allow flexion, extension and bending of the cervical spine.
