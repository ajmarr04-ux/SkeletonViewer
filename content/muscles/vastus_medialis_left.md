Vastus medialis forms the teardrop-shaped bulge above and inside the knee. It arises from the intertrochanteric line, the medial lip of the linea aspera and the medial supracondylar line, and inserts on the medial border of the patella and the quadriceps tendon. It extends the knee, and its lowest, almost horizontal fibres hold the patella against a lateral pull.
