Inferior oblique is the only extra-ocular muscle that does not arise at the apex of the orbit. It arises from the orbital surface of the maxilla in the floor of the orbit, just lateral to the nasolacrimal canal, and passes backwards and laterally beneath the eyeball to the sclera. It elevates the eye, mainly when it is adducted, and also abducts and extorts it.
