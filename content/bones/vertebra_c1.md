The atlas is the first cervical vertebra, a ring of bone with no body and no spinous process. Its lateral masses carry the skull on concave superior facets, forming the atlanto-occipital joints for nodding. Its anterior arch turns around the dens of the axis, which gives most of the head's rotation.
