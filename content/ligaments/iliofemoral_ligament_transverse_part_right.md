The transverse part of the iliofemoral ligament is the upper, more horizontal limb of the Y-shaped ligament at the front of the hip. It arises from the anterior inferior iliac spine and the acetabular rim and passes laterally to the upper part of the intertrochanteric line of the femur. It tightens in extension and limits the hip from extending and rotating outwards.
