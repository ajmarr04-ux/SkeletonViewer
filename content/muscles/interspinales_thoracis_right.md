The interspinales thoracis are small and inconstant, usually found only between the spinous processes of the first and second and the eleventh and twelfth thoracic vertebrae, where the spine is more mobile. Each lies beside the interspinous ligament. They steady adjacent vertebrae and probably act mainly as position sensors rather than movers.
