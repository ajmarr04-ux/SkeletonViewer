The anterior meniscotibial ligament of the lateral meniscus is a short fibrous band, sometimes called the anterior horn attachment. It fixes the front end of the lateral meniscus to the anterior intercondylar area of the tibia. It keeps the anterior horn in place on the tibial plateau, so that the meniscus stays correctly positioned as the femoral condyle rolls and glides across it.
