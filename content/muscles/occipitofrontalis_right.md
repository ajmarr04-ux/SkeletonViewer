Occipitofrontalis is the main muscle of the scalp. Its frontal and occipital bellies are joined by the epicranial aponeurosis, a tough sheet that slides over the pericranium. The occipital belly anchors the aponeurosis behind; the frontal belly, with no bony attachment, pulls on the skin of the forehead, raising the eyebrows and wrinkling the brow in surprise or attention.
