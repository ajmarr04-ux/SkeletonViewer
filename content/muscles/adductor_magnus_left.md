Adductor magnus is the largest and deepest of the adductors, a great triangular sheet from the pubis and ischium to almost the whole length of the femur. Its adductor part, including the upper portion called adductor minimus, adducts the thigh. Its hamstring part, from the ischial tuberosity to the adductor tubercle, also extends the hip and has a hamstring's nerve supply.
