The iliolumbar ligament is a strong, fan-shaped band running from the tips of the transverse processes of the fourth and fifth lumbar vertebrae to the iliac crest and the adjacent ala of the ilium. It binds the lower lumbar spine to the pelvis and helps to resist forward slipping and twisting of the fifth lumbar vertebra.
