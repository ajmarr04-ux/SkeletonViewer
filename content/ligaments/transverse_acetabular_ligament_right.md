The transverse acetabular ligament is a strong band that crosses the acetabular notch at the lower edge of the hip socket, converting the notch into a foramen. It is continuous with the labrum of the acetabulum on either side. Small vessels enter the joint beneath it, and the ligament of the head of the femur is attached to its margins.
