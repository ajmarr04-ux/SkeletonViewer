Triceps brachii is the only muscle of the posterior compartment of the arm and the main extensor of the elbow. Its long, lateral and medial heads arise from the scapula and the back of the humerus and join in a common tendon on the olecranon of the ulna. The long head also crosses the shoulder, where it helps extend and adduct the arm and steadies the head of the humerus.
