Gluteus medius is a broad, fan-shaped muscle on the outer surface of the ilium, partly under gluteus maximus. Its fibres converge on the lateral surface of the greater trochanter. It abducts the hip, its front fibres rotate it medially, and in walking it holds the pelvis level each time the opposite foot leaves the ground.
