The middle phalanx of the index finger lies between the proximal and distal phalanges. Its base has two shallow hollows that fit the head of the proximal phalanx at the proximal interphalangeal joint, and its head meets the distal phalanx. Flexor digitorum superficialis splits to insert on the sides of its shaft, and the central slip of the extensor tendon inserts on the back of its base.
