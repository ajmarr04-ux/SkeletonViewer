Zygomaticus minor arises from the lateral surface of the zygomatic bone, in front of and medial to zygomaticus major, and passes downwards and medially into the muscle of the upper lip, lateral to levator labii superioris. It raises the upper lip, exposing the upper teeth, and deepens the nasolabial furrow, contributing to smiling and to expressions of sadness.
