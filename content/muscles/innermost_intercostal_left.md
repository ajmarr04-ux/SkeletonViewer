The innermost intercostal muscles are the deepest layer of the lateral parts of the intercostal spaces, separated from the internal intercostals by the intercostal nerves and vessels. Their fibres run in the same direction as the internal intercostals, between the internal surfaces of adjacent ribs. They are probably best regarded as part of the internal intercostals and act with them.
