The dorsal radio-ulnar ligament is a band on the back of the wrist that strengthens the capsule of the distal radio-ulnar joint. It passes between the dorsal margin of the ulnar notch of the radius and the head and styloid region of the ulna. It holds the lower ends of the two bones together as the radius turns around the ulna.
