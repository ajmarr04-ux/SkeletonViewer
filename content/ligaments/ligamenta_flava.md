The ligamenta flava are paired, yellow, elastic sheets joining the laminae of adjacent vertebrae, from the second cervical vertebra down to the first segment of the sacrum. They form part of the back wall of the vertebral canal. Their elastic tissue keeps them under slight tension, limits flexion and helps the trunk return upright after bending.
