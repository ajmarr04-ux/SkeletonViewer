The dorsal calcaneocuboid ligament is a thin band on the upper surface of the foot. It runs from the dorsal surface of the anterior part of the calcaneus to the dorsal surface of the cuboid. It reinforces the capsule of the calcaneocuboid joint from above and, with the calcaneonavicular ligament, forms the bifurcate ligament in some descriptions.
