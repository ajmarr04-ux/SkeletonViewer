The lunotriquetral interosseous ligament is a short, strong band that joins the lunate to the triquetrum, between their adjoining surfaces in the proximal row of the carpus. It closes the proximal side of the joint between them and binds the two bones, so that they move together when the wrist deviates towards the ulnar or radial side.
