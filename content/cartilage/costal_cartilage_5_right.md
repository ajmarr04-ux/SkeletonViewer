The fifth costal cartilage continues the front end of the fifth rib and runs medially to the side of the body of the sternum, forming a synovial sternocostal joint. Like the other true-rib cartilages it connects the rib directly to the breastbone. Its elasticity allows the chest wall to expand and spring back during breathing.
