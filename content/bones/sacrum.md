The sacrum is a triangular bone formed by five fused sacral vertebrae, wedged between the hip bones at the back of the pelvis. It passes the weight of the body to the pelvis through the sacro-iliac joints. Its promontory marks the back of the pelvic inlet, and the sacral nerves leave through four pairs of foramina.
