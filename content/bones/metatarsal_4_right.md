The fourth metatarsal is shorter than the third. Its base articulates with the cuboid and the lateral cuneiform behind and with the third and fifth metatarsals at its sides. Its head carries the proximal phalanx of the fourth toe. It is more mobile than the second and third metatarsals, letting the outer forefoot adapt to the ground.
