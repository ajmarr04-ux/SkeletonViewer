Obturator internus arises inside the pelvis from the inner surface of the obturator membrane and the bone around it, forming part of the side wall of the pelvis. Its tendon turns sharply around the lesser sciatic notch and, flanked by the two gemelli, inserts on the medial surface of the greater trochanter. It rotates the extended hip laterally and abducts the flexed hip.
