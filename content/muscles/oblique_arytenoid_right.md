Oblique arytenoid lies on the back of transverse arytenoid, crossing its fellow to form an X. Each runs from the muscular process of one arytenoid cartilage to the apex of the other, and some fibres continue as the ary-epiglottic part in the ary-epiglottic fold. It draws the arytenoids together and narrows the inlet of the larynx, acting as a sphincter in swallowing.
