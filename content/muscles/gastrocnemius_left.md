Gastrocnemius is the most superficial calf muscle and gives the calf its shape. Its two heads arise from the femur just above the condyles and join in a belly that ends in the calcaneal tendon, shared with soleus, on the back of the calcaneus. It plantarflexes the ankle, powerfully in running and jumping, and helps flex the knee.
