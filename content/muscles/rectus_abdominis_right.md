Rectus abdominis is the long, strap-like muscle beside the midline of the front of the abdomen, enclosed in the rectus sheath. It arises from the pubic crest and the pubic symphysis and ascends to the xiphoid process and the fifth to seventh costal cartilages. Three or more tendinous intersections, fixed to the front of the sheath, divide it into segments. It flexes the trunk and compresses the abdomen.
