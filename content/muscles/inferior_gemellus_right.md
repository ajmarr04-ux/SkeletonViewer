Inferior gemellus is a small muscle arising from the upper part of the ischial tuberosity. It runs laterally along the lower border of the obturator internus tendon and blends with it, inserting with that tendon on the medial surface of the greater trochanter. Like its partner above, it helps rotate the extended hip laterally and steadies the head of the femur.
