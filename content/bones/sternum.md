The sternum is the flat bone at the front of the chest. It has three parts, joined in this model into one object: the manubrium, the body and the small xiphoid process. The clavicles articulate with the manubrium, and the costal cartilages of the upper seven ribs join its sides. The sternal angle marks the second rib.
