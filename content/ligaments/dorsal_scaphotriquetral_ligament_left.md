The dorsal scaphotriquetral ligament is a band on the back of the wrist that joins the scaphoid to the triquetrum, passing across the dorsal surface of the lunate. It is described as part of the dorsal intercarpal ligament system. It helps to link the radial and ulnar sides of the carpus and to stabilise the midcarpal joint.
