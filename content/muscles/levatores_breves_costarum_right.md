The levatores costarum breves are twelve small, fan-shaped muscles on each side. Each arises from the tip of the transverse process of C7 or of T1 to T11 and passes obliquely downwards and laterally to the outer surface of the rib immediately below, between its tubercle and angle. They raise the ribs slightly and help bend the spine to the same side.
