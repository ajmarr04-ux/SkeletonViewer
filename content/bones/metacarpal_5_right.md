The fifth metacarpal, in the little finger, articulates with the hamate at its base and with the fourth metacarpal beside it. A tubercle on the medial side of the base receives extensor carpi ulnaris. Its joint with the hamate is the most mobile of the four finger metacarpals, letting the edge of the hand curl forward when gripping.
