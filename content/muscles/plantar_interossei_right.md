The three plantar interossei lie under the third, fourth and fifth metatarsals, each arising from the base and medial side of one bone. They insert on the medial sides of the bases of the proximal phalanges of the same toes. They adduct the third to fifth toes towards the line of the second toe and help flex the metatarsophalangeal joints.
