The ischiofemoral ligament is the thin, spiralling thickening of the hip capsule at the back of the joint. It arises from the ischial part of the acetabular rim and sweeps upwards and laterally over the femoral neck to blend with the capsule near the base of the greater trochanter. It tightens in extension and medial rotation, helping to keep the femoral head seated.
