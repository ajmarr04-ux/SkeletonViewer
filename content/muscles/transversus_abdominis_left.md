Transversus abdominis is the innermost flat muscle of the abdominal wall, its fibres running mostly horizontally. It arises from the inner surfaces of the lower six costal cartilages, the thoracolumbar fascia, the iliac crest and the lateral inguinal ligament, and ends in an aponeurosis that reaches the linea alba and, through the conjoint tendon, the pubic crest. It compresses the abdomen like a corset and steadies the lumbar spine.
