Fibularis tertius is a small, inconstant muscle, often regarded as the lowest part of extensor digitorum longus. It arises from the lower medial surface of the fibula and the interosseous membrane, and its tendon inserts on the dorsal surface of the base of the fifth metatarsal. It helps dorsiflex the ankle and evert the foot.
