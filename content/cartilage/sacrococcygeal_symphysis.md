The sacrococcygeal symphysis is the cartilaginous joint between the apex of the sacrum and the base of the first coccygeal segment. A thin, flattened fibrocartilaginous disc lies between the two bones and is reinforced by ligaments in front, behind and at the sides. It permits a small amount of movement, which lets the coccyx be pushed backwards during childbirth.
