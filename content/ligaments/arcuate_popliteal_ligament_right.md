The arcuate popliteal ligament is a Y-shaped thickening of the posterior capsule of the knee, arching over the tendon of popliteus. It arises from the back of the head of the fibula and spreads upwards and medially, blending with the oblique popliteal ligament and the capsule over the lateral femoral condyle. It strengthens the back of the capsule on the lateral side.
