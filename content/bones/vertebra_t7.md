The seventh thoracic vertebra is a typical thoracic vertebra, with a heart-shaped body and the longest, most steeply sloping spinous processes of the column around it. Demifacets on its body take the heads of ribs 7 and 8, and its transverse costal facets take the tubercles of rib 7.
