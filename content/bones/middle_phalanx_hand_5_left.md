The middle phalanx of the little finger is the smallest of the middle phalanges of the hand. Its base articulates with the proximal phalanx, and its head with the distal phalanx. Flexor digitorum superficialis inserts on its sides, though in some people the superficialis tendon to the little finger is thin or absent.
