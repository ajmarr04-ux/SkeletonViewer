The second metacarpal, in the index finger, is the longest metacarpal. Its notched base locks between the trapezium, trapezoid and capitate and also meets the third metacarpal, so it barely moves. Its head forms the knuckle of the index finger and carries the proximal phalanx. Extensor carpi radialis longus and flexor carpi radialis insert on its base.
