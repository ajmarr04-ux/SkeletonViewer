Geniohyoid is a narrow, strap-like muscle lying above mylohyoid, next to its fellow in the midline. It runs from the inferior mental spine on the inner surface of the mandible back to the front of the body of the hyoid. It pulls the hyoid forwards and upwards, widening the pharynx for swallowing, and helps open the mouth when the hyoid is held still.
