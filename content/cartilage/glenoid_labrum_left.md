The glenoid labrum is a rim of fibrocartilage fixed around the margin of the glenoid cavity of the scapula. It is triangular in section and blends above with the tendon of the long head of biceps. It deepens the shallow socket and increases the contact area for the head of the humerus, adding to the stability of the shoulder.
