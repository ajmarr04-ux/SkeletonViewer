The lacrimal bone is the smallest and most fragile bone of the face, a thin plate at the front of the medial wall of the orbit. With the frontal process of the maxilla it forms the lacrimal fossa, which holds the lacrimal sac, and the start of the nasolacrimal canal, which drains tears into the nose.
