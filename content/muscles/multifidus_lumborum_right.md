Multifidus lumborum is the thickest part of multifidus, filling the groove beside the lumbar spinous processes. Its fascicles arise from the back of the sacrum, the posterior superior iliac spine and the mammillary processes of the lumbar vertebrae, and each passes up two to four segments to a spinous process. It stabilises the lumbar segments and extends the lumbar spine.
