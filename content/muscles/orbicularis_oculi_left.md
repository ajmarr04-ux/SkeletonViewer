Orbicularis oculi is the sphincter of the eyelids, a flat ring of muscle around the orbital margin and within the lids. Its orbital part closes the eyelids tightly, as in squinting or protecting the eye; its palpebral part closes them gently in blinking and sleep, and its deep lacrimal fibres help draw tears towards the lacrimal sac.
