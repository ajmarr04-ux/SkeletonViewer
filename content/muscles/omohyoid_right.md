Omohyoid is a long, thin muscle with two bellies joined by an intermediate tendon, which is bound to the clavicle by a fascial sling. The inferior belly arises from the upper border of the scapula near the suprascapular notch and crosses the lower neck; the superior belly runs up to the lower border of the body of the hyoid. It depresses and steadies the hyoid.
