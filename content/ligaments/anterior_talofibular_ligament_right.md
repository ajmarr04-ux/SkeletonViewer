The anterior talofibular ligament is a flat band of the lateral collateral ligament of the ankle. It runs forwards and medially from the anterior margin of the lateral malleolus of the fibula to the neck of the talus, in front of its lateral articular surface. It is the weakest of the lateral ligaments and resists the talus sliding forwards and turning inwards.
