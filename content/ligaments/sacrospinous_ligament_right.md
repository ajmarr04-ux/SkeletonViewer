The sacrospinous ligament is a thin, triangular band that runs from the side of the lower sacrum and coccyx to the ischial spine, lying in front of the sacrotuberous ligament. With that ligament it turns the greater and lesser sciatic notches into foramina, and it resists upward tilting of the lower end of the sacrum.
