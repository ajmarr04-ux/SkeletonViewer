Longus capitis is a broad, thick band in front of the upper cervical vertebrae, under cover of the pharynx. It arises by tendinous slips from the anterior tubercles of the transverse processes of C3 to C6 and runs up to the inferior surface of the basilar part of the occipital bone. It flexes the head on the neck and helps flex the upper cervical spine.
