The external intercostal membrane is a thin fibrous sheet in the intercostal spaces between the costal cartilages, filling the gap in front where the external intercostal muscle ends. It runs obliquely downwards and medially, in line with the muscle fibres, between adjacent ribs and costal cartilages as far as the sternum, and closes the front of each space.
