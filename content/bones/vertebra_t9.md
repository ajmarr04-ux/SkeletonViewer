The ninth thoracic vertebra has the shape of a typical thoracic vertebra. Demifacets above take the heads of rib 9, and its transverse costal facets take the tubercles of rib 9. The demifacet below, for rib 10, is often missing, because rib 10 usually articulates with T10 alone.
