The interosseous membrane of the leg is a thin, strong fibrous sheet stretched between the interosseous borders of the tibia and fibula. Its fibres mostly run downwards and laterally. It holds the two bones together, transmits some load from the fibula to the tibia, and gives attachment to deep muscles of the anterior and posterior compartments. An opening at the top lets the anterior tibial vessels pass forwards.
