Extensor digitorum brevis is a thin muscle on the top of the foot, seen as a soft swelling in front of the lateral malleolus. It arises from the upper surface of the calcaneus and divides into tendons that join the long extensor tendons of the second to fourth toes, reaching their middle and distal phalanges through the extensor expansions. It helps extend these toes.
