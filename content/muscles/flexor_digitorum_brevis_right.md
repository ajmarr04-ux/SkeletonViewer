Flexor digitorum brevis lies in the middle of the sole, immediately above the plantar aponeurosis. It arises from the medial process of the calcaneal tuberosity and divides into four tendons, each splitting to let a tendon of flexor digitorum longus through before inserting on the sides of a middle phalanx of the second to fifth toes. It flexes these toes and supports the arch.
