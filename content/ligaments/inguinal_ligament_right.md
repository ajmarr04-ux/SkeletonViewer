The inguinal ligament is a band running along the groin from the anterior superior iliac spine to the pubic tubercle. It is the rolled-under lower edge of the aponeurosis of the external oblique muscle. It forms the floor of the inguinal canal, gives attachment to the muscles of the abdominal wall and fascia of the thigh, and serves as a landmark between the abdomen and the thigh.
