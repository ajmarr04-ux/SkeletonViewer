Rectus femoris is the only part of the quadriceps that crosses the hip. Its straight head arises from the anterior inferior iliac spine and its reflected head from the ilium just above the acetabulum. It runs down the middle of the front of the thigh to the base of the patella and, through the patellar ligament, the tibial tuberosity. It extends the knee and flexes the hip.
