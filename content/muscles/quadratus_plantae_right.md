Quadratus plantae is a flat, square muscle in the second layer of the sole. It arises by two heads from the medial and lateral sides of the plantar surface of the calcaneus and inserts into the tendon of flexor digitorum longus. It straightens the oblique pull of that tendon and so helps flex the second to fifth toes.
