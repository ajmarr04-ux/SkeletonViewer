The ulnar collateral ligament of the wrist is a rounded cord on the little-finger side of the joint. It runs from the styloid process of the ulna to the triquetrum, with some fibres continuing to the pisiform. It stabilises the ulnar side of the wrist and resists radial deviation, working with the radial collateral ligament on the opposite side.
