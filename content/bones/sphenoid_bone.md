The sphenoid is a butterfly-shaped bone at the centre of the skull base, touching every other cranial bone. Its body contains the sphenoidal sinus and carries the sella turcica, which holds the pituitary gland. Its greater and lesser wings and its pterygoid processes carry openings for cranial nerves and anchor muscles of chewing.
