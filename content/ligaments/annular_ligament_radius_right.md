The annular ligament is a strong band that forms about four fifths of a ring around the head and neck of the radius. Its ends attach to the anterior and posterior margins of the radial notch of the ulna. It holds the radial head against the ulna while letting it spin freely, and it is continuous with the capsule of the elbow.
