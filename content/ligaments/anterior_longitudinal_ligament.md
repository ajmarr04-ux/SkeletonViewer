The anterior longitudinal ligament is a broad, strong band covering the front of the vertebral bodies and intervertebral discs. It runs from the basilar part of the occipital bone and the anterior tubercle of the atlas down to the front of the sacrum. It is firmly attached to the discs and the edges of the bodies, and it resists hyperextension of the spine.
