Rectus capitis lateralis is a short, flat muscle that continues the line of the intertransverse muscles to the skull. It arises from the upper surface of the transverse process of the atlas and inserts into the inferior surface of the jugular process of the occipital bone. It tilts the head to its own side and steadies it on the atlas.
