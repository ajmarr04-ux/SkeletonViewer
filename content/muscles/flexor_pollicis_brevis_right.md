Flexor pollicis brevis lies medial to abductor pollicis brevis in the thenar eminence. Its superficial head arises from the flexor retinaculum and trapezium, and its smaller deep head from the trapezoid and capitate; both insert, over the radial sesamoid, on the base of the proximal phalanx of the thumb. It flexes the metacarpophalangeal joint of the thumb and helps in opposition.
