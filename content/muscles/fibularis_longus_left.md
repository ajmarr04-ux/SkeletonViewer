Fibularis longus is the superficial muscle of the lateral leg. It arises from the head and upper lateral surface of the fibula; its tendon passes behind the lateral malleolus, turns under the cuboid in a groove and crosses the sole to the medial cuneiform and the base of the first metatarsal. It everts the foot, helps plantarflex the ankle and supports the arches.
