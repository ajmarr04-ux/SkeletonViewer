The scaphoid is the largest bone of the proximal carpal row, shaped like a boat, on the thumb side of the wrist. It articulates with the radius above, and with the lunate, capitate, trapezium and trapezoid. Because it bridges the two carpal rows, it takes much of the load when someone falls on an outstretched hand.
