The intercuneiform interosseous ligaments are strong short fibres that lie deep between the adjoining sides of the three cuneiform bones. One set joins the medial and intermediate cuneiforms, and another joins the intermediate and lateral cuneiforms. They bind the bones tightly, allowing very little movement, and contribute to the transverse arch of the midfoot.
