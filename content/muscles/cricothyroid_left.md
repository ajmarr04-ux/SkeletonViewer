Cricothyroid is the only intrinsic muscle of the larynx on its outer surface and the only one not supplied by the recurrent laryngeal nerve. It arises from the front and side of the cricoid arch in straight and oblique parts that fan out to the thyroid cartilage. It tilts the thyroid cartilage forwards on the cricoid, lengthening and tensing the vocal folds and raising the pitch of the voice.
