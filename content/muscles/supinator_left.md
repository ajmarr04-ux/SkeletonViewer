Supinator is a broad sheet of muscle that wraps around the upper third of the radius, deep in the back of the forearm. It arises from the lateral epicondyle, the radial collateral and annular ligaments and the supinator crest of the ulna, and inserts on the lateral, back and front surfaces of the proximal radius. It supinates the forearm, alone when slow and unresisted, and with biceps against resistance.
