The dorsal intercuneiform ligaments are two short transverse bands on the upper surface of the midfoot. One connects the medial and intermediate cuneiforms, and the other connects the intermediate and lateral cuneiforms, running across their dorsal surfaces. They strengthen the intercuneiform joints and help to maintain the transverse arch of the foot.
