The superior glenohumeral ligament is a slender band in the upper part of the shoulder capsule. It runs from the upper rim of the glenoid cavity of the scapula, near the base of the coracoid process, to the humerus near the lesser tubercle. It lies beside the coracohumeral ligament and helps to stop the humeral head dropping downwards.
