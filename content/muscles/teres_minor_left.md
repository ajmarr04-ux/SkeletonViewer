Teres minor is a narrow, elongated muscle along the upper part of the lateral border of the scapula, often fused with infraspinatus above it. It inserts on the lowest facet of the greater tubercle of the humerus. It laterally rotates the arm with infraspinatus and, as part of the rotator cuff, holds the head of the humerus in the glenoid cavity.
