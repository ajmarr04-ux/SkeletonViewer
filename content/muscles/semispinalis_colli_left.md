Semispinalis colli (semispinalis cervicis) is thicker than semispinalis thoracis and lies deep to semispinalis capitis. It arises from the transverse processes of the upper five or six thoracic vertebrae and ascends to the spinous processes of C2 to C5; its largest slip ends on the axis. It extends the neck and turns it to the opposite side.
