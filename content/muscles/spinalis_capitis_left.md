Spinalis capitis is a slender, often indistinct muscle that is usually inseparable from semispinalis capitis. It arises from the spinous processes of C7 and T1, sometimes with fibres from the cervical transverse processes, and inserts with semispinalis capitis into the occipital bone between the superior and inferior nuchal lines. It extends the head.
