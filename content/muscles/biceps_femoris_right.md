Biceps femoris is the lateral hamstring. Its long head arises from the ischial tuberosity and its short head from the back of the femur, and they join in a tendon that forms the outer border of the popliteal fossa and inserts on the head of the fibula. It flexes the knee and rotates the flexed knee laterally; the long head also extends the hip.
