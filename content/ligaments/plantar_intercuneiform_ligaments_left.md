The plantar intercuneiform ligaments are transverse bands on the sole of the foot. They connect the plantar surfaces of neighbouring cuneiform bones, linking the medial to the intermediate and the intermediate to the lateral cuneiform. They hold the three cuneiforms together and help to maintain the transverse arch of the midfoot.
