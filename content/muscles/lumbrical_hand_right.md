The four lumbricals are slender, worm-like muscles in the palm, unusual in arising from tendons rather than bone: from the tendons of flexor digitorum profundus. Each passes along the radial side of a finger to its extensor expansion. They flex the knuckles while extending the interphalangeal joints, the position used to hold a pen or play the piano.
