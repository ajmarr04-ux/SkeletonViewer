The fourth cervical vertebra is a typical cervical vertebra, with a small body, uncinate processes, a large triangular vertebral foramen and a short bifid spinous process. The tubercles of its transverse processes anchor the scalene muscles and the deep neck flexors, and the vertebral artery passes through its transverse foramina.
