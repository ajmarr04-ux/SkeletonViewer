The nuchal ligament is a broad, sheet-like band in the midline at the back of the neck, the upward continuation of the supraspinous ligament. It runs from the vertebra prominens (C7) to the external occipital protuberance and crest, attaching to the spinous processes of the cervical vertebrae and the posterior tubercle of the atlas. It limits flexion and gives attachment to muscles.
