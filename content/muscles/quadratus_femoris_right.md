Quadratus femoris is a flat, rectangular muscle below the inferior gemellus. It runs horizontally from the lateral border of the ischial tuberosity to the quadrate tubercle on the intertrochanteric crest of the femur. It is a strong lateral rotator of the hip and helps hold the head of the femur in the acetabulum.
