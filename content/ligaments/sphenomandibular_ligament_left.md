The sphenomandibular ligament is a flat, thin band on the inner side of the temporomandibular joint. It runs from the spine of the sphenoid bone to the lingula of the mandible, close to the mandibular foramen. It is a remnant of the first pharyngeal arch cartilage and acts as a passive suspension for the lower jaw during opening and closing.
