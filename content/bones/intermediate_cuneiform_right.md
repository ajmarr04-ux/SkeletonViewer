The intermediate cuneiform is the smallest of the three cuneiforms. It sits between the medial and lateral cuneiforms, behind the second metatarsal and in front of the navicular. Because it is shorter than its neighbours, the base of the second metatarsal is set back in a recess between them, which locks the middle of the foot.
