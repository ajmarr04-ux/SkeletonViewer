Levator scapulae is a strap-like muscle at the side and back of the neck, mostly hidden by sternocleidomastoid and trapezius. It arises from the transverse processes of the atlas and axis and the posterior tubercles of C3 and C4, and descends to the medial border of the scapula between the superior angle and the root of the spine. It raises the scapula and tilts the glenoid cavity down.
