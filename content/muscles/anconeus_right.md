Anconeus is a small, triangular muscle at the back of the elbow, partly blended with the medial head of triceps. It runs from the back of the lateral epicondyle of the humerus to the lateral side of the olecranon and the upper posterior surface of the ulna. It helps triceps extend the elbow, steadies the joint, and may abduct the ulna during pronation.
