Tensor fasciae latae is a short muscle at the front of the hip, between two layers of the fascia lata. It arises from the anterior superior iliac spine and the front of the iliac crest and inserts into the iliotibial tract, which carries its pull down to the tibia. It helps flex, abduct and medially rotate the hip, and steadies the extended knee.
