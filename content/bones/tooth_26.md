The upper first molar is the largest upper tooth and the first permanent tooth to erupt, at about six years, behind the milk teeth. Its crown has four main cusps joined by an oblique ridge, sometimes with a fifth small cusp, and it has three roots, two buccal and one palatal, in the maxilla.
