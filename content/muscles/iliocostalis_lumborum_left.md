Iliocostalis lumborum is the lowest and most lateral column of erector spinae. It arises from the broad common tendon attached to the sacrum, the iliac crest and the lumbar spinous processes, and its slips insert into the angles of the lower six or seven ribs and, in its lumbar part, the lumbar transverse processes. It extends the lumbar spine and bends it to the same side.
