Flexor digitorum profundus is a bulky muscle covering the front of the ulna and the interosseous membrane. Its four tendons pass through the carpal tunnel deep to the superficial flexor tendons, through their split, to the bases of the distal phalanges. It is the only muscle that flexes the distal interphalangeal joints, and it goes on to flex the other finger joints and the wrist. The lumbricals arise from its tendons.
