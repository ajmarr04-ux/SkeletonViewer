The pubofemoral ligament is a triangular thickening of the front and lower part of the hip capsule. It arises from the superior ramus of the pubis and the obturator crest and passes laterally and downwards to blend with the capsule and the lower part of the iliofemoral ligament. It becomes taut in abduction and extension, limiting both movements.
