Obliquus capitis superior arises from the upper surface of the transverse process of the atlas and passes upwards and backwards to the occipital bone between the superior and inferior nuchal lines, lateral to rectus capitis posterior major. It forms the lateral side of the suboccipital triangle. It extends the head and tilts it to its own side.
