The anterior sacro-iliac ligament is a thin sheet of fibres that forms part of the front of the capsule of the sacro-iliac joint. It runs between the anterior surface of the lateral part of the sacrum and the adjacent margin of the ilium, and it is the weakest of the three ligaments that support this joint.
