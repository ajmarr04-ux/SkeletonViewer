Depressor anguli oris is a triangular muscle of the lower lip region. It arises by a broad base from the oblique line of the mandible, below the canine, premolar and first molar teeth, and converges on the modiolus at the angle of the mouth. It pulls the corner of the mouth downwards and laterally, as in sadness or opening the mouth wide.
