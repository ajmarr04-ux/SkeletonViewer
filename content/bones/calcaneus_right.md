The calcaneus is the heel bone, the largest bone of the foot. Its back forms the heel, where the calcaneal (Achilles) tendon inserts. The talus rests on its upper surface at the subtalar joint, supported on a shelf, the sustentaculum tali. In front, it articulates with the cuboid. It forms the back of the arches of the foot.
