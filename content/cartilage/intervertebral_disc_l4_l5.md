The L4-L5 intervertebral disc lies between the bodies of two adjacent lumbar vertebrae, joining them. A soft gelatinous nucleus pulposus is enclosed by thick fibrous rings, the anulus fibrosus. Lumbar discs are the thickest in the spine, which suits their role in bearing the weight of the upper body and in allowing bending and extension of the lower back.
