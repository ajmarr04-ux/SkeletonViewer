The articular disc of the temporomandibular joint is an oval plate of fibrous tissue between the head of the mandible and the mandibular fossa of the temporal bone. Its edge blends with the joint capsule. It divides the joint into an upper compartment, for gliding, and a lower one, for hinge movement, and fits the two bones together.
