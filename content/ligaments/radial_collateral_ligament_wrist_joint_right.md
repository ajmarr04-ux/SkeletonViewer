The radial collateral ligament of the wrist is a short, strong band on the thumb side of the joint. It passes from the tip of the radial styloid process to the scaphoid, with some fibres reaching the trapezium. The radial artery lies just in front of it. It stabilises the radial side of the wrist and resists ulnar deviation.
