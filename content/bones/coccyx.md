The coccyx is the small, triangular tailbone at the bottom of the vertebral column, usually formed by four fused rudimentary vertebrae. Its base articulates with the apex of the sacrum. It anchors the muscles and ligaments of the pelvic floor and part of the gluteus maximus, and helps support the pelvic organs.
