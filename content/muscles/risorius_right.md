Risorius is a narrow, superficial and variable band of muscle that runs horizontally across the cheek. It arises from the fascia over the parotid gland and masseter, has no bony attachment, and converges on the modiolus at the angle of the mouth. It draws the corner of the mouth laterally, as in grinning or a strained smile; in some people it is absent.
