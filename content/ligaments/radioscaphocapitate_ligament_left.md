The radioscaphocapitate ligament is part of the palmar radiocarpal complex on the front of the wrist. It arises from the radial styloid process, runs across the waist of the scaphoid, which it supports like a sling, and ends on the capitate. It strengthens the palmar capsule and helps to resist forward displacement of the carpus.
