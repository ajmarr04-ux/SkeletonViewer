Rectus capitis posterior minor is a small triangular muscle beside the midline, medial to rectus capitis posterior major. It arises from the posterior tubercle of the atlas and widens as it ascends to the medial part of the inferior nuchal line and the occipital bone below it. It extends the head, and its connection to the spinal dura mater may help keep the dura from folding.
