The anterior talocalcaneal ligament is a small band that joins the neck of the talus to the upper surface of the calcaneus, in front of the sinus tarsi. It lies on the anterior side of the subtalar joint and holds the two bones together there. It is sometimes described as a thickening of the joint capsule and is often indistinct.
