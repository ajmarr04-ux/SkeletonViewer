Deltoid is the thick, triangular muscle that caps the shoulder and gives it its rounded outline. Its clavicular, acromial and spinal parts arise in a curve from the clavicle, acromion and spine of the scapula, mirroring the insertion of trapezius, and converge on the deltoid tuberosity of the humerus. The acromial part abducts the arm beyond the first few degrees; the front and back parts flex and extend it.
