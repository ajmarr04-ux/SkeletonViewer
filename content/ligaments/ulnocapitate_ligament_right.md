The ulnocapitate ligament is a palmar ligament on the ulnar side of the wrist. It passes from the ulnar side of the distal forearm, at the triangular fibrocartilage complex, distally and radially to the capitate. It lies deep to the palmar radiocarpal ligaments and helps to support the front of the wrist, linking the ulnar side of the forearm to the carpus.
