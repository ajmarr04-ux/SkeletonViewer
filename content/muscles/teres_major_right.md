Teres major is a thick, rounded muscle that runs from the back of the inferior angle of the scapula to the crest of the lesser tubercle of the humerus, beside the tendon of latissimus dorsi. It adducts, medially rotates and extends the arm, acting as a "little helper" of latissimus dorsi, and forms the lower border of the quadrangular and triangular spaces.
