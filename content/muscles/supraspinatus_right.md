Supraspinatus fills the supraspinous fossa of the scapula and passes under the acromion and the coraco-acromial arch to the top facet of the greater tubercle of the humerus. As part of the rotator cuff it holds the head of the humerus in the shallow glenoid cavity, and it starts abduction of the arm before deltoid takes over.
