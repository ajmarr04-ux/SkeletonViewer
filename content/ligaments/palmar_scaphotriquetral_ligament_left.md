The palmar scaphotriquetral ligament is a band on the front of the wrist that links the scaphoid to the triquetrum, crossing in front of the capitate and lunate across the midcarpal joint. It reinforces the palmar capsule and helps to stabilise the midcarpal joint, tying the radial and ulnar sides of the carpus together.
