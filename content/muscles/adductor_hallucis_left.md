Adductor hallucis lies deep in the sole, in its third layer. Its oblique head comes from the bases of the middle metatarsals and its transverse head from the plantar ligaments of the lateral toes, and both insert, with the lateral head of flexor hallucis brevis, on the lateral side of the base of the proximal phalanx of the big toe. It adducts the big toe and helps support the transverse arch.
