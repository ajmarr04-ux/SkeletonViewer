Extensor digitorum is the main extensor of the fingers, lying in the middle of the back of the forearm. It arises from the lateral epicondyle by the common extensor tendon and divides into four tendons, linked by bands on the back of the hand. Each forms an extensor expansion over a finger, inserting on the middle and distal phalanges. It extends the knuckles chiefly, and the finger joints and wrist with help.
