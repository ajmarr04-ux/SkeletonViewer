The stylomandibular ligament is a thickened band of deep cervical fascia running from the apex of the styloid process of the temporal bone to the angle and posterior border of the ramus of the mandible. It is an accessory ligament of the temporomandibular joint, limiting protrusion, and it separates the parotid region from the submandibular region.
