The anterior tibiofibular ligament is a flat band at the front of the distal tibiofibular syndesmosis. It runs obliquely downwards and laterally from the anterior border of the lower end of the tibia to the front of the lateral malleolus. It holds the lower ends of the two bones together and helps to maintain the grip of the ankle mortise on the talus.
