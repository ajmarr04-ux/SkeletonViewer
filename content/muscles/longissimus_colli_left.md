Longissimus colli (longissimus cervicis) lies medial to longissimus thoracis at its upper end. It arises by long tendons from the transverse processes of the upper four or five thoracic vertebrae and ascends to the posterior tubercles of the transverse processes of C2 to C6. It extends the neck and bends it to the same side.
