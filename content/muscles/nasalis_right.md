Nasalis arises from the maxilla just lateral to the incisive fossa, over the canine and lateral incisor teeth. Its transverse part spreads over the bridge of the nose into an aponeurosis shared with its fellow; its alar part runs to the alar cartilage. The transverse part compresses the nasal aperture, and the alar part draws the ala down and outwards, widening the nostril.
