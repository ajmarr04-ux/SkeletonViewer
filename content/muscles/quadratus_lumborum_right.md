Quadratus lumborum is a thick quadrilateral muscle in the posterior abdominal wall, beside the lumbar vertebrae. It arises from the posterior part of the iliac crest and the iliolumbar ligament and ascends to the medial half of the lower border of the twelfth rib and the tips of the upper four lumbar transverse processes. It bends the lumbar spine to the same side and fixes the twelfth rib in inspiration.
