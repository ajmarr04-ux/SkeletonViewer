The plantar calcaneocuboid ligament, also called the short plantar ligament, is a short, wide and strong band on the sole of the foot. It runs from the anterior tubercle of the calcaneus to the plantar surface of the cuboid behind its ridge, lying deep to the long plantar ligament. It supports the lateral longitudinal arch of the foot.
