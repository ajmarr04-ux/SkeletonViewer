The stapes is the smallest bone in the body, shaped like a stirrup. Its head articulates with the incus, and its two limbs lead to an oval base that sits in the oval window of the inner ear, sealed by the annular ligament. Its piston-like movement passes sound vibrations into the fluid of the cochlea.
