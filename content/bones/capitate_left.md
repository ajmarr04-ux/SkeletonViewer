The capitate is the largest carpal bone and sits in the centre of the wrist. Its rounded head fits into the hollow formed by the scaphoid and lunate, and its base articulates mainly with the third metacarpal, as well as the second and fourth. At its sides it meets the trapezoid and hamate. Most wrist movements turn around it.
