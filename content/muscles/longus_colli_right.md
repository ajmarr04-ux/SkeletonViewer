Longus colli lies on the front of the vertebral column from the atlas to the third thoracic vertebra. It has three parts: a vertical part between the vertebral bodies, a superior oblique part to the anterior tubercle of the atlas, and an inferior oblique part from the upper thoracic bodies to the transverse processes of C5 and C6. It flexes the neck and supports the cervical lordosis.
