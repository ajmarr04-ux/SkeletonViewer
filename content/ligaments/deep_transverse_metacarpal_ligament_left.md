The deep transverse metacarpal ligament consists of three short bands that cross the palm side of the heads of the second to fifth metacarpals, joining the palmar plates of neighbouring metacarpophalangeal joints. It holds the metacarpal heads together and limits how far they can spread, while the interossei pass behind it.
