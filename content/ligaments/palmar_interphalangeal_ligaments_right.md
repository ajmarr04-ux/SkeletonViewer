The palmar ligaments of the interphalangeal joints, also called palmar plates, are thick fibrocartilaginous plates on the palm side of each finger joint. Each is attached firmly to the base of the more distal phalanx and loosely to the proximal one. They prevent hyperextension of the joint and form a smooth floor for the flexor tendons.
