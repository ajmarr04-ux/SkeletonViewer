The lateral ligament is a strong thickening of the joint capsule on the outer side of the temporomandibular joint. Its fibres run from the articular tubercle and zygomatic process of the temporal bone obliquely downwards and backwards to the lateral surface of the neck of the mandible. It reinforces the capsule and restrains backward movement of the mandibular head.
