Pyramidalis is a small triangular muscle in front of the lower end of rectus abdominis, inside the rectus sheath. It arises from the front of the pubis and the pubic symphysis and narrows as it ascends to the linea alba, about midway between the pubis and the umbilicus. It tenses the linea alba. It is often absent on one or both sides.
