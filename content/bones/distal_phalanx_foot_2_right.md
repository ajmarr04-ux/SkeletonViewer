The distal phalanx of the second toe forms the tip of the toe. Its base articulates with the middle phalanx at the distal interphalangeal joint, and its end widens into a small tuberosity that supports the nail and the pulp. Flexor digitorum longus inserts on its sole surface, and the extensor tendon on the back of its base.
