The inferior pubic ligament is a thick arch of fibres that forms the lower margin of the pubic symphysis. It runs between the inferior pubic rami of the two sides, filling the angle of the pubic arch and rounding off its apex. It is blended with the fibrocartilaginous disc and strengthens the joint on its lower side.
