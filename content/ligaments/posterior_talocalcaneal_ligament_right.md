The posterior talocalcaneal ligament is a short band at the back of the subtalar joint. It runs from the lateral tubercle of the posterior process of the talus to the upper and medial part of the calcaneus. It strengthens the posterior part of the joint capsule and helps to hold the talus on the calcaneus.
