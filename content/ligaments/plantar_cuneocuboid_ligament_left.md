The plantar cuneocuboid ligament is a strong transverse band on the sole of the foot. It joins the plantar surface of the cuboid to the plantar surface of the lateral cuneiform, bridging the cuneocuboid joint. It holds the two bones together on the underside of the midfoot and helps to support the transverse arch.
