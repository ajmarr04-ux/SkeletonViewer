The posterior sacro-iliac ligament is a strong band covering the interosseous ligament at the back of the sacro-iliac joint. Its short fibres pass from the lateral sacral crest and the posterior sacral segments to the iliac tuberosity and posterior iliac spines, and its long fibres run to the posterior superior iliac spine. It holds the sacrum in place.
