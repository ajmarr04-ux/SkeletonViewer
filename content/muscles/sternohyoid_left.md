Sternohyoid is a thin, strap-like muscle beside the midline of the front of the neck. It arises from the back of the manubrium and the medial end of the clavicle and runs up to the lower border of the body of the hyoid. It depresses the hyoid after it has been raised in swallowing and speech, and steadies it as a base for the tongue.
