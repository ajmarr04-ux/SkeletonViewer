The twelfth thoracic vertebra is a transitional vertebra. Its upper facets are thoracic, but its lower articular processes face outwards like those of a lumbar vertebra. A single facet on each side of its body takes the head of rib 12, and its transverse processes are small, with mammillary and accessory tubercles.
