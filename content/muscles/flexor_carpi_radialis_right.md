Flexor carpi radialis arises from the medial epicondyle of the humerus by the common flexor tendon. Its long tendon, a guide to the radial artery lateral to it at the wrist, passes through its own groove in the trapezium to the bases of the second and third metacarpals. It flexes the wrist and, with the radial extensors, abducts it (radial deviation).
