The interclavicular ligament is a band of fibres that passes from the upper part of the sternal end of one clavicle to the other. It crosses the top of the manubrium of the sternum and is attached to the jugular notch. It strengthens the upper part of the sternoclavicular joints and helps to hold up the shoulders.
