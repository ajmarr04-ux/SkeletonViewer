The external anal sphincter is the voluntary sphincter of the anal canal, a ring of skeletal muscle around its lower two-thirds. It has subcutaneous, superficial and deep parts: the superficial part is anchored in front to the perineal body and behind, through the anococcygeal ligament, to the coccyx; the deep part blends with puborectalis. It keeps the anal canal closed and relaxes during defecation.
