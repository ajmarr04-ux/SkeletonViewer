The inferior nasal concha is a thin, scroll-shaped bone on the side wall of the nasal cavity. Unlike the superior and middle conchae, it is a separate bone rather than part of the ethmoid. Covered with richly vascular lining, it warms and moistens inhaled air, and the nasolacrimal duct opens beneath it.
