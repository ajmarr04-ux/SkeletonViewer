The radiocapitate ligament is one of the strong palmar radiocarpal bands on the front of the wrist. It arises from the anterior margin of the radial styloid process and the distal radius and runs distally and medially across the carpus to the capitate. It reinforces the palmar capsule and helps to stop the carpus slipping forwards.
