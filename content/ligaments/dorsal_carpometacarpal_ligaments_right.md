The dorsal carpometacarpal ligaments are bands on the back of the hand that run from the distal row of carpal bones to the bases of the second to fifth metacarpals. They strengthen the carpometacarpal joints and hold the metacarpal bases to the carpus. They are stronger and more numerous than the palmar set.
