- `intercornual_ligament_left`: The source object is a left-sided "intercornual ligament"; TA2 lists it as the lateral sacrococcygeal ligament (Ligamenta sacrococcygea lateralia), which is what I used for `latin`. Textbooks differ on whether the intercornual ligaments are a separate name for the same band.
- `anterior_longitudinal_ligament`, `posterior_longitudinal_ligament`, `interspinous_ligaments`, `ligamenta_flava`, `supraspinous_ligament`, `nuchal_ligament`: `joint` is the functional `vertebral_column` set (the nuchal ligament is `null`, which is on the list). `connects` lists the end attachments (or every vertebra it spans for the segmental ligaments), not intermediate vertebrae for the longitudinal ligaments.
- `external_intercostal_membrane_left`, `internal_intercostal_membrane_left`: `connects` lists ribs 1 to 12 on the left, since the membranes lie in the spaces between them; costal cartilages (for the external membrane) are not listed.
- `costotransverse_ligament_left`, `intra_articular_ligament_head_rib_left`, `radiate_ligament_head_rib_left`: `joint` is the functional `costovertebral_joints_left`, as the structure spans several pair joints. The intra-articular ligament is listed for ribs 2 to 9 (and T1 to T9), as ribs 1 and 10 to 12 meet a single vertebra; some texts say 2 to 10.
- `anterior_longitudinal_ligament`: attaches to the occipital bone's basilar part and the atlas's anterior tubercle in Gray's/Moore's; I listed `occipital_bone` and `vertebra_c1` plus `sacrum`. Other texts start it at the axis.
- `stylomandibular_ligament_left`: Given `joint: temporomandibular_joint_left` as an accessory ligament of the TMJ, though some texts treat it as a thickening of cervical fascia not belonging to the joint; it isn't on the no-joint list.
