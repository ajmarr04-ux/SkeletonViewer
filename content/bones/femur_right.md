The femur is the bone of the thigh and the longest bone in the body. Its rounded head fits deep into the acetabulum of the hip bone, set on an angled neck. The greater and lesser trochanters anchor the hip muscles. At the knee, its two large condyles rest on the tibia, and the patella glides in the groove between them.
