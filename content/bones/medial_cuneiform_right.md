The medial cuneiform is the largest of the three wedge-shaped cuneiform bones. It articulates with the navicular behind, the intermediate cuneiform beside it, and the bases of the first and second metatarsals in front. Tibialis anterior and fibularis longus both insert on it, so it acts as a stirrup that supports the medial arch.
