The posterior tibiotalar ligament is the deepest and strongest part of the medial (deltoid) ligament of the ankle. It runs from the medial malleolus of the tibia, behind and below the other deltoid parts, to the medial surface and medial tubercle of the talus. It resists eversion and excessive plantarflexion of the talus.
