The medial meniscus is a C-shaped crescent of fibrocartilage on the medial condyle of the tibia, wider behind than in front. Its horns are anchored in the intercondylar area, and its outer margin is firmly attached to the joint capsule and the deep part of the tibial collateral ligament. Thick at the rim and thin at its free inner edge, it deepens the socket for the femoral condyle.
