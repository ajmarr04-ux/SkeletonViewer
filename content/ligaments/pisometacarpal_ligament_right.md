The pisometacarpal ligament is a strong band that runs from the pisiform to the base of the fifth metacarpal. Like the pisohamate ligament, it is in effect a continuation of the tendon of flexor carpi ulnaris, which is attached to the pisiform. It holds the pisiform in place and carries the pull of the muscle to the fifth metacarpal.
