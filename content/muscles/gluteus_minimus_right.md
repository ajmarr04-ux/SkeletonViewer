Gluteus minimus is the smallest and deepest of the three gluteal muscles, lying beneath gluteus medius on the outer surface of the ilium. Its fibres end in a tendon on the front of the greater trochanter. It abducts and medially rotates the hip and, with gluteus medius, keeps the pelvis level when the body stands on one leg.
