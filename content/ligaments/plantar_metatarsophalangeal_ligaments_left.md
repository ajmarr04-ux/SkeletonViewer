The plantar metatarsophalangeal ligaments are thick fibrocartilaginous plates, also called plantar plates, in the floor of each metatarsophalangeal joint. Each is firmly attached to the base of the proximal phalanx and loosely to the neck of the metatarsal. They strengthen the capsule, resist overextension and provide a gliding groove for the flexor tendons.
