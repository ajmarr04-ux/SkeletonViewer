The hyoid is a U-shaped bone in the front of the neck, between the mandible and the larynx, at about the level of the third cervical vertebra. It articulates with no other bone: it hangs from the styloid processes by ligaments and is held by muscles. It anchors the tongue and moves in swallowing and speech.
