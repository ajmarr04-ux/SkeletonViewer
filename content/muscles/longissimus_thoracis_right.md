Longissimus thoracis is the largest part of erector spinae, the intermediate column. It arises from the common tendon on the sacrum and iliac crest and from the lumbar spinous and transverse processes, and inserts into the transverse processes of all the thoracic vertebrae and the lower nine or ten ribs between their tubercles and angles. It extends the thoracic and lumbar spine and bends it to the same side.
