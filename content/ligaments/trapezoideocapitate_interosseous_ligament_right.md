The trapezoideocapitate interosseous ligament is a short, strong band joining the neighbouring surfaces of the trapezoid and the capitate in the distal row of carpal bones. It lies within the joint between them and holds the two bones tightly together, adding to the rigid transverse arch formed by the distal carpal row.
