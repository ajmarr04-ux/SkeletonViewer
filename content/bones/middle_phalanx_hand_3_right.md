The middle phalanx of the middle finger is the longest of the middle phalanges. Its base articulates with the proximal phalanx at the proximal interphalangeal joint, a hinge, and its head with the distal phalanx. The two slips of flexor digitorum superficialis insert on the sides of its shaft and bend the finger at the middle joint.
