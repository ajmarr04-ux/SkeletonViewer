The articular disc of the distal radio-ulnar joint, often called the triangular fibrocartilage, is a triangular plate. Its base is attached to the lower edge of the ulnar notch of the radius and its apex to the base of the ulnar styloid process. It binds the two bones together and separates the joint from the wrist cavity.
