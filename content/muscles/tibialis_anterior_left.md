Tibialis anterior is the large muscle beside the shin, felt as the tendon at the front of the ankle. It arises from the lateral condyle and upper lateral surface of the tibia and the interosseous membrane, and inserts on the medial cuneiform and the base of the first metatarsal. It is the main dorsiflexor of the ankle and inverts the foot.
