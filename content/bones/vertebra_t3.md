The third thoracic vertebra is a typical thoracic vertebra, with a heart-shaped body, a small round vertebral foramen and a long, downward-sloping spinous process. Demifacets on its body take the heads of ribs 3 and 4, and its transverse costal facets take the tubercles of rib 3.
