The costotransverse ligament is a short, strong band that fills the narrow space between the back of the neck of a rib and the front of the transverse process of the vertebra behind it. It is present at ribs 1 to 10 and anchors the rib to the vertebra, strengthening the costotransverse joint and the rib's attachment to the vertebral column.
