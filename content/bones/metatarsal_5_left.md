The fifth metatarsal lies along the outer edge of the foot. Its base articulates with the cuboid and the fourth metatarsal, and projects backwards as a tuberosity that can be felt halfway along the edge of the foot. Fibularis brevis inserts on the tuberosity. Its head carries the proximal phalanx of the little toe.
