Serratus posterior superior is a thin, quadrilateral muscle deep to the rhomboids. It arises from the lower part of the nuchal ligament and the spinous processes of C7 to T3, and its four digitations run down and laterally to the upper borders of the second to fourth ribs, just lateral to their angles. It has been thought to raise the ribs, but its role in breathing is doubtful; it may act mainly in proprioception.
