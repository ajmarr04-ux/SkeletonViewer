The fifth rib is a typical rib, its head articulating with T4 and T5 and its tubercle with the transverse process of T5. It lies at the level of the lower heart and the dome of the diaphragm, and the external oblique muscle of the abdomen begins on its outer surface. Its cartilage joins the sternum.
