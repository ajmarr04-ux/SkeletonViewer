The trapezoid ligament is the lateral, flat, four-sided part of the coracoclavicular ligament. It passes from the upper surface of the coracoid process of the scapula obliquely upwards to the trapezoid line on the underside of the clavicle. It lies in front of and lateral to the conoid ligament and helps to bind the scapula to the clavicle.
