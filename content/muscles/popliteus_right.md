Popliteus is a thin, triangular muscle in the floor of the popliteal fossa. It arises by a tendon from the lateral condyle of the femur, inside the knee capsule, with fibres to the lateral meniscus, and inserts on the back of the tibia above the soleal line. It unlocks the fully extended knee by rotating the femur laterally on the tibia.
