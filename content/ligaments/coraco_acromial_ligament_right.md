The coraco-acromial ligament is a strong triangular band that joins the lateral border of the coracoid process to the tip of the acromion, both parts of the scapula. Together with these two processes it forms an arch over the head of the humerus and the supraspinatus tendon, protecting the shoulder joint from above.
