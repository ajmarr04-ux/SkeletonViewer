The ulnopisiform ligament is a small band on the ulnar side of the front of the wrist. It is described as running from the ulnar styloid region and triangular fibrocartilage complex to the pisiform. It lies with the other palmar ulnocarpal ligaments and adds to the support of the ulnar side of the wrist capsule.
