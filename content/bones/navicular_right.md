The navicular is a boat-shaped bone on the medial side of the foot, between the talus and the three cuneiforms. Its concave back receives the head of the talus, and its front has three facets for the cuneiforms. Its tuberosity can be felt in front of the medial malleolus and receives the main insertion of tibialis posterior.
