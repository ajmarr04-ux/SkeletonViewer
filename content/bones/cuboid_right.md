The cuboid is the cube-shaped bone on the lateral side of the foot. It articulates with the calcaneus behind, the lateral cuneiform medially, and the bases of the fourth and fifth metatarsals in front. A groove on its sole carries the tendon of fibularis longus across the foot. It forms part of the lateral arch.
