Lateral pterygoid is a short, thick muscle with two heads lying horizontally in the infratemporal fossa. Its fibres run backwards to the neck of the mandible and the capsule and articular disc of the temporomandibular joint. It pulls the head of the mandible and the disc forwards onto the articular tubercle, protruding the jaw and starting the opening of the mouth; acting alternately, the two sides grind.
