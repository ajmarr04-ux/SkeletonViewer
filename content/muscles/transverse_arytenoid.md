Transverse arytenoid is the only unpaired intrinsic muscle of the larynx. It spans the back of the two arytenoid cartilages, running horizontally from the posterior surface of one to the other, behind the oblique arytenoids. It draws the arytenoids together, closing the posterior, intercartilaginous part of the rima glottidis, and so helps close the glottis in phonation and swallowing.
