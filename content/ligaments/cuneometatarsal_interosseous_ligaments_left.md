The cuneometatarsal interosseous ligaments are strong bands deep within the tarsometatarsal joints. The medial one runs from the lateral side of the medial cuneiform to the base of the second metatarsal. The lateral one runs from the lateral cuneiform to the adjacent sides of the bases of the third and fourth metatarsals. They bind the tarsus to the metatarsus.
