The intra-articular ligament is a short band inside the joint of the head of a rib. It runs from the crest of the head of the rib to the intervertebral disc between the two vertebrae with which the rib articulates, dividing the joint cavity into upper and lower compartments. It is present in ribs 2 to 9 where the head meets two vertebrae, and absent in ribs 1, 10, 11 and 12.
