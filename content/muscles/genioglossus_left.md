Genioglossus is a fan-shaped extrinsic muscle that makes up most of the bulk of the tongue. It arises from the superior mental spine on the inner surface of the mandible and spreads backwards into the tongue from tip to root, its lowest fibres reaching the body of the hyoid. It protrudes the tongue, depresses its centre and, with its fellow, keeps the tongue from falling back.
