Levator anguli oris lies deep to levator labii superioris and zygomaticus minor. It arises from the canine fossa of the maxilla, just below the infra-orbital foramen, and runs down to the modiolus at the angle of the mouth, where it mingles with zygomaticus major, depressor anguli oris and orbicularis oris. It raises the corner of the mouth and deepens the nasolabial furrow, as in smiling.
