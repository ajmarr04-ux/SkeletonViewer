The palmar carpometacarpal ligaments are bands on the palm side of the hand that pass from the distal row of carpal bones to the bases of the second to fifth metacarpals. They are generally thinner than the dorsal set. They reinforce the front of the carpometacarpal joint capsules and help to keep the metacarpal bases in place.
