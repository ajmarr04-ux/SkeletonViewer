Flexor carpi ulnaris is the most medial superficial flexor of the forearm. Its humeral and ulnar heads are joined by a tendinous arch under which the ulnar nerve enters the forearm. The tendon inserts on the pisiform and continues through the pisohamate and pisometacarpal ligaments to the hook of the hamate and the base of the fifth metacarpal. It flexes and adducts (ulnar deviates) the wrist.
