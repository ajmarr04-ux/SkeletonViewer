The L5-S1 intervertebral disc lies between the body of the fifth lumbar vertebra and the base of the sacrum, at the lumbosacral junction. A soft gelatinous nucleus pulposus is enclosed by thick fibrous rings, the anulus fibrosus. The disc is wedge-shaped, thicker in front than behind, which helps to produce the angle between the lumbar spine and sacrum, and it carries the weight of the trunk into the pelvis.
