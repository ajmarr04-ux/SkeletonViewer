The second lumbar vertebra is a typical lumbar vertebra, with a large, kidney-shaped body, short thick pedicles, a broad, hatchet-shaped spinous process and long, thin transverse processes. The crura of the diaphragm and the psoas major muscle attach to the front and sides of its body.
