Vastus intermedius lies deep to rectus femoris, between the other two vasti, and arises from the front and lateral surfaces of the upper two thirds of the femoral shaft. Its fibres end in the deep layer of the quadriceps tendon on the base of the patella, and through the patellar ligament it extends the knee.
