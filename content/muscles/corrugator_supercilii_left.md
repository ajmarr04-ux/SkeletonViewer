Corrugator supercilii is a small, narrow muscle lying deep to the frontal belly of occipitofrontalis and orbicularis oculi. It arises from the medial end of the superciliary arch and passes laterally and upwards into the skin above the middle of the eyebrow. It draws the eyebrows downwards and medially, producing the vertical frown lines above the nose and shading the eyes from bright light.
