The articular disc of the sternoclavicular joint is a flat plate of fibrocartilage that completely divides the joint into two cavities. It is attached above to the clavicle and below to the first costal cartilage, near its junction with the manubrium, and blends with the capsule. It helps stop the clavicle sliding medially and absorbs forces.
