The distal phalanx of the ring finger supports the pulp and the nail at the fingertip. Its base articulates with the middle phalanx at the distal interphalangeal joint. The deep flexor tendon, flexor digitorum profundus, inserts on the palm side of its base, and the terminal extensor tendon on the back.
