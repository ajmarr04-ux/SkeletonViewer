The first metatarsal, behind the big toe, is the shortest and thickest metatarsal. Its base articulates with the medial cuneiform, and its large head with the proximal phalanx of the big toe. Two sesamoid bones in the flexor hallucis brevis tendons glide in grooves under the head (they are not part of this model). It bears much of the load during push-off.
