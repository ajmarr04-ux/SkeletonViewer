Psoas major is a long, thick muscle on either side of the lumbar spine. It arises from the bodies, discs and transverse processes of T12 to L5, runs down along the pelvic brim beneath the inguinal ligament, and joins iliacus in a common tendon on the lesser trochanter of the femur. Together they form iliopsoas, the strongest flexor of the hip.
