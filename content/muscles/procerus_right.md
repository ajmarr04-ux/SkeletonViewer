Procerus is a small pyramidal slip of muscle on the bridge of the nose, continuous above with the frontal belly of occipitofrontalis. It arises from the fascia over the lower nasal bone and the upper lateral nasal cartilage and inserts into the skin between the eyebrows. It draws the medial ends of the eyebrows down and wrinkles the skin over the bridge of the nose transversely.
