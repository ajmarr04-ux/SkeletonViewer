Subscapularis is the large, triangular muscle on the front of the scapula, filling the subscapular fossa and forming much of the posterior wall of the axilla. Its tendon crosses in front of the shoulder joint to the lesser tubercle of the humerus. It is the chief medial rotator of the arm and the front member of the rotator cuff, resisting forward dislocation of the humeral head.
