The talocalcaneal interosseous ligament is a strong, thick band in the sinus tarsi and tarsal canal, between the talus and calcaneus. It runs from the groove on the underside of the talus to the groove on the upper surface of the calcaneus. It separates the subtalar joint from the talocalcaneonavicular joint and is a major stabiliser of the subtalar joint.
