The proximal phalanx of the ring finger lies between the fourth metacarpal and the middle phalanx. Its concave base meets the metacarpal head at the knuckle, and its pulley-shaped head meets the middle phalanx. The flexor tendons run in a fibrous sheath along its palm surface, and interosseous muscles insert at its base.
