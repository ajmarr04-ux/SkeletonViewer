The middle phalanx of the little toe is very small. Its base articulates with the proximal phalanx and its head with the distal phalanx. In many people it is fused with the distal phalanx, so the little toe has only two separate phalanges. A slip of flexor digitorum brevis inserts on it, though this slip is often missing.
