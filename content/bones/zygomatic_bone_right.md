The zygomatic bone forms the prominence of the cheek and part of the lateral wall and floor of the orbit. Its temporal process joins the zygomatic process of the temporal bone to make the zygomatic arch, and its frontal process rises to meet the frontal bone. The masseter muscle arises from its lower border.
