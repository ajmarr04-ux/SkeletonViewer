Flexor digitorum superficialis is the largest superficial muscle of the forearm, lying between the superficial and deep flexor layers. Its humero-ulnar and radial heads join over the median nerve, and four tendons pass through the carpal tunnel. Each splits at the proximal phalanx to let the deep tendon through, then inserts on the sides of the middle phalanx. It flexes the proximal interphalangeal joints, then the knuckles and wrist.
