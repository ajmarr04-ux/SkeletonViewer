The proximal phalanx of the fourth toe lies between the fourth metatarsal and the middle phalanx. Its concave base fits the metatarsal head, and its head forms the proximal interphalangeal joint. The fourth dorsal and second plantar interossei insert on its base and move the toe away from and towards the second toe.
