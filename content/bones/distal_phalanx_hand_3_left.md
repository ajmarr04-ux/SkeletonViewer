The distal phalanx of the middle finger is the terminal bone of that finger. Its base meets the head of the middle phalanx at the distal interphalangeal joint. The end of the bone spreads into a horseshoe-shaped tuberosity under the nail bed and the pulp. Flexor digitorum profundus inserts on the palm side of its base, and the extensor tendon on the back.
