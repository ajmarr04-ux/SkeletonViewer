Iliacus is the fan-shaped muscle filling the iliac fossa on the inner surface of the ilium. Its fibres converge beneath the inguinal ligament, most joining the lateral side of the psoas tendon on the lesser trochanter of the femur. With psoas major it forms iliopsoas, which flexes the hip and, with the thigh fixed, bends the trunk forwards, as in sitting up.
