The lateral meniscus is a nearly circular crescent of fibrocartilage on the lateral condyle of the tibia, smaller and more mobile than the medial meniscus. Its horns are anchored close together in the intercondylar area, and it is not attached to the fibular collateral ligament. It deepens the socket for the femoral condyle and shares load.
