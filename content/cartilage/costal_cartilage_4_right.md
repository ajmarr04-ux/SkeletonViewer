The fourth costal cartilage continues the front end of the fourth rib and runs medially to the side of the body of the sternum, where it forms a synovial sternocostal joint. As a true-rib cartilage it joins its rib to the breastbone directly. Its elasticity lets the chest wall expand and recoil in breathing.
