Spinalis colli (spinalis cervicis) is an inconstant muscle of the back of the neck. When present, it arises from the lower part of the nuchal ligament and the spinous processes of C7 and the upper one or two thoracic vertebrae, and inserts into the spinous process of the axis and sometimes C3 and C4. It extends the neck.
