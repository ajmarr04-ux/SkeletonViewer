The first metacarpal, in the thumb, is the shortest and thickest of the metacarpals. Its saddle-shaped base articulates with the trapezium, and, unlike the other metacarpals, it does not usually meet its neighbour, so it moves freely. It is turned so that its palm surface faces the other fingers, which allows opposition. Its head carries the proximal phalanx of the thumb.
