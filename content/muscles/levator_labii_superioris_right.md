Levator labii superioris is a flat sheet arising from the infra-orbital margin of the maxilla and the adjacent zygomatic bone, above the infra-orbital foramen. Its fibres converge downwards into the muscular substance of the upper lip, between levator nasolabialis and zygomaticus minor. It raises and everts the upper lip and helps deepen the nasolabial furrow in expressions of sadness or seriousness.
