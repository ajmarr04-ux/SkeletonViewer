Iliocostalis thoracis is the middle part of the iliocostalis column. It arises by tendons from the upper borders of the angles of the lower six ribs, medial to the insertions of iliocostalis lumborum, and inserts into the angles of the upper six ribs and the transverse process of C7. It extends the thoracic spine, bends it to the same side and helps keep the back erect.
