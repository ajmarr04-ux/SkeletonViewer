Temporoparietalis is a thin, variable sheet of muscle on the side of the scalp, between the frontal belly of occipitofrontalis and the anterior and superior auricular muscles. It arises from the lateral part of the epicranial aponeurosis and passes down to the root of the auricle. It tightens the scalp and may raise the ear slightly; in many people it is barely developed.
