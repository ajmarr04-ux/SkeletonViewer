Iliocostalis colli (iliocostalis cervicis) is the uppermost part of the iliocostalis column. It arises from the angles of the third to sixth ribs, medial to iliocostalis thoracis, and inserts into the posterior tubercles of the transverse processes of C4 to C6. It extends the lower neck and bends it to the same side.
