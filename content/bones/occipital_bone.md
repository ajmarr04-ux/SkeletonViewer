The occipital bone forms the back and much of the base of the skull. Its large opening, the foramen magnum, transmits the medulla oblongata, and the occipital condyles beside it rest on the atlas at the atlanto-occipital joints, allowing nodding. Its outer surface anchors the neck muscles along the nuchal lines.
