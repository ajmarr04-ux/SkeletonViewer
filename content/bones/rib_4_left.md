The fourth rib is a typical rib. Its head articulates with T3 and T4 and its tubercle with the transverse process of T4. The shaft curves forwards from a sharp angle at the back, and its lower border carries the costal groove, which protects the intercostal vein, artery and nerve. Its cartilage joins the sternum.
