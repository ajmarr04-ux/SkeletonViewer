The second metatarsal is usually the longest metatarsal. Its base sits in a recess between the three cuneiforms, articulating with all of them and with the third metatarsal, which holds it almost still. Its head carries the proximal phalanx of the second toe. It forms the central, stiff axis of the forefoot.
