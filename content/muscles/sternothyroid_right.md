Sternothyroid is wider and shorter than sternohyoid, under which it lies. It arises from the back of the manubrium, below the origin of sternohyoid, and from the first costal cartilage, and runs up to the oblique line on the lamina of the thyroid cartilage. It draws the larynx downwards after it has been raised in swallowing and speech.
