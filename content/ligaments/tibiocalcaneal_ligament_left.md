The tibiocalcaneal ligament is a part of the superficial layer of the medial (deltoid) ligament of the ankle. It descends almost vertically from the apex of the medial malleolus to the sustentaculum tali of the calcaneus. It lies over both the ankle and subtalar joints and helps to resist eversion of the heel.
