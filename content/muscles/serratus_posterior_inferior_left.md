Serratus posterior inferior is a thin, broad muscle at the junction of the thoracic and lumbar regions, deep to latissimus dorsi. It arises from the spinous processes of T11 to L2 through the thoracolumbar fascia and its four digitations pass up and laterally to the inferior borders of the lower four ribs near their angles. It draws the lower ribs down and back, though its role in breathing is doubtful.
