Gracilis is the long, slender, most superficial muscle of the medial thigh. It arises from the body and inferior ramus of the pubis and runs straight down to the upper medial surface of the tibia, between the tendons of sartorius and semitendinosus in the pes anserinus. It adducts the thigh and helps flex and medially rotate the knee.
