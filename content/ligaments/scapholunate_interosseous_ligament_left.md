The scapholunate interosseous ligament is a short, strong C-shaped band that joins the adjacent margins of the scaphoid and lunate in the proximal carpal row. It is thickest at its dorsal and palmar parts and thin in the middle. It closes the joint space between the two bones and keeps them moving together at the wrist.
