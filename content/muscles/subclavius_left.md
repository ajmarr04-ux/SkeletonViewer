Subclavius is a small, rounded muscle beneath the clavicle. It arises from the junction of the first rib and its costal cartilage and runs laterally to the groove on the underside of the middle third of the clavicle. It anchors and depresses the clavicle, steadying it during movements of the shoulder, and its bulk may cushion the subclavian vessels when the clavicle is fractured.
