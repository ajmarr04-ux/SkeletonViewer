The trapeziotrapezoidal interosseous ligament is a short, strong band that joins the adjoining surfaces of the trapezium and the trapezoid in the distal row of carpal bones. It lies within the joint between them and holds the two bones firmly together, so that they act as a stable unit at the base of the index finger and thumb.
