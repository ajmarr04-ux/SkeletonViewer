The dorsal intercarpal ligaments are the short bands that join neighbouring carpal bones on the back of the wrist. They run transversely or obliquely between bones of the same row and between the two rows. They bind the carpus into a stable arch and allow only limited gliding between bones, while reinforcing the dorsal joint capsule.
