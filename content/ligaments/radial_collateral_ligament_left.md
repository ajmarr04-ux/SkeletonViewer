The radial collateral ligament is a fan-shaped band on the lateral side of the elbow. It arises from the lateral epicondyle of the humerus and spreads downwards to blend with the annular ligament of the radius. It stabilises the elbow against varus force and helps to keep the radial head against the humerus.
