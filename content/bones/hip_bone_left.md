The hip bone is the large, irregular bone of the pelvis. It forms from three bones, the ilium, ischium and pubis, which fuse in the acetabulum in the teenage years. It articulates with the sacrum at the sacroiliac joint, with the other hip bone at the pubic symphysis, and with the head of the femur in the acetabulum.
