Fibularis brevis lies beneath fibularis longus and arises from the lower two thirds of the lateral surface of the fibula. Its tendon runs in front of the longus tendon behind the lateral malleolus and inserts on the tuberosity at the base of the fifth metatarsal. It everts the foot and helps plantarflex the ankle, guarding against inversion sprains.
