Piriformis is a pear-shaped muscle that arises inside the pelvis from the front of the sacrum, between the sacral foramina. It leaves through the greater sciatic foramen and inserts on the upper border of the greater trochanter. It rotates the extended hip laterally, helps abduct the flexed hip and holds the head of the femur in the acetabulum.
