The proximal phalanx of the second toe is the first of its three bones. Its concave base articulates with the head of the second metatarsal at the metatarsophalangeal joint, and its head with the middle phalanx. The first and second dorsal interossei insert on either side of its base, so it is the reference toe for spreading the toes.
