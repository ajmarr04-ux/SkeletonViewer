The supraspinous ligament is a strong cord running along the tips of the spinous processes from the seventh cervical vertebra down to the sacrum. It is thickest in the lumbar region and blends with the interspinous ligaments in front and the thoracolumbar fascia behind. Above C7 it continues as the nuchal ligament. It resists flexion of the spine.
