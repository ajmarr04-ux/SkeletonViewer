The anterior ligament of the fibular head is made up of two or three broad, flat bands. They run obliquely upwards and medially from the front of the head of the fibula to the front of the lateral tibial condyle. They reinforce the capsule of the proximal tibiofibular joint, bind the fibula to the tibia and allow only slight gliding movement of the fibular head.
