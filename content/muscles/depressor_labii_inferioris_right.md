Depressor labii inferioris is a small quadrilateral muscle that arises from the oblique line of the mandible, between the mental symphysis and the mental foramen, partly under cover of depressor anguli oris. Its fibres pass upwards and medially to blend with orbicularis oris and its fellow in the skin of the lower lip, which it draws downwards and laterally, as in expressions of irony or distaste.
