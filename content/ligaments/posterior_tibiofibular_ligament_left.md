The posterior tibiofibular ligament lies at the back of the distal tibiofibular syndesmosis. It passes obliquely downwards and laterally from the posterior tubercle at the lower end of the tibia to the back of the lateral malleolus. It is stronger than the anterior ligament and resists backward displacement of the fibula, helping to keep the ankle mortise firm around the talus.
