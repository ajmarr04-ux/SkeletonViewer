The third lumbar vertebra is a typical lumbar vertebra, with a large, kidney-shaped body and the longest transverse processes of the lumbar spine. Its broad spinous process and the mammillary processes on its superior articular processes anchor the deep back muscles. It sits at the summit of the lumbar curve.
