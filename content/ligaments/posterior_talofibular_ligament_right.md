The posterior talofibular ligament is the strongest and deepest of the lateral ligaments of the ankle. It runs almost horizontally backwards and medially from the malleolar fossa on the medial surface of the lateral malleolus to the lateral tubercle of the posterior process of the talus. It holds the talus against the fibula and checks backward slipping and extreme dorsiflexion.
