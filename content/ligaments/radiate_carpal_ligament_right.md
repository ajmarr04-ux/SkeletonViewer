The radiate carpal ligament fans out on the palmar surface of the wrist from the head of the capitate to the scaphoid, lunate and triquetrum. It is part of the palmar ligaments that cross the midcarpal joint, and it binds the two rows of carpal bones together. It strengthens the front of the wrist and helps to limit extension.
