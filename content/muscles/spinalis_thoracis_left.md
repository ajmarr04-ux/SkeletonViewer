Spinalis thoracis is the medial and smallest column of erector spinae, closely applied to the spinous processes. It arises from the spinous processes of T11 to L2 and inserts into the spinous processes of the upper thoracic vertebrae, from T1 to about T8. It extends the thoracic spine. It is often blended with longissimus thoracis and hard to separate from it.
