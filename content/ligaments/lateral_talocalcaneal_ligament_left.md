The lateral talocalcaneal ligament is a short band on the lateral side of the subtalar joint. It runs from the lateral surface of the talus, below the lateral malleolus, to the lateral surface of the calcaneus, and lies parallel to the calcaneofibular ligament. It strengthens the joint capsule and helps to limit movement of the heel.
