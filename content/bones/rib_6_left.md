The sixth rib is a typical rib, its head articulating with T5 and T6 and its tubercle with the transverse process of T6. Its long, curved shaft sweeps round the side of the chest, and its costal cartilage joins the sternum near the xiphoid process, giving attachment to the rectus abdominis.
