External oblique is the largest and most superficial of the three flat muscles of the abdominal wall. It arises by digitations from the outer surfaces of the lower eight ribs, interlocking with serratus anterior and latissimus dorsi, and its fibres run downwards and medially into a broad aponeurosis that reaches the linea alba, the pubic tubercle and the iliac crest. Its folded lower border forms the inguinal ligament.
