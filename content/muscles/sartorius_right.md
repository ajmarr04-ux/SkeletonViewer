Sartorius is the longest muscle in the body, a narrow strap that runs from the anterior superior iliac spine obliquely across the front of the thigh to the upper medial surface of the tibia. It crosses both the hip and the knee, and helps flex, abduct and laterally rotate the hip and flex the knee, the movements of sitting cross-legged.
