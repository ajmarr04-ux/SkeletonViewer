Hyoglossus is a thin quadrilateral sheet on the side of the tongue. It arises from the body and the whole length of the greater horn of the hyoid and passes upwards into the side and underside of the tongue, blending with styloglossus. It depresses and retracts the tongue. It is a key landmark: the lingual nerve and hypoglossal nerve lie on it, the lingual artery deep to it.
