The meniscopatellar ligaments are fibrous bands within the front of the knee capsule, part of the patellar retinacula. They run from the sides of the patella down and back to the anterior borders of the medial and lateral menisci. They link the kneecap to the menisci and are thought to draw the menisci forwards when the knee is extended.
