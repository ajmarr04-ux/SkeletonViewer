The middle phalanx of the third toe is short and nearly as wide as it is long. Its base meets the proximal phalanx at the proximal interphalangeal joint, and its head meets the distal phalanx. The tendon of flexor digitorum brevis inserts on the sides of its sole surface and bends the toe at the middle joint.
