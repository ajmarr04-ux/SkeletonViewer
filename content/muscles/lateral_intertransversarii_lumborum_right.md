The lateral intertransversarii lumborum are small muscles between the transverse processes of adjacent lumbar vertebrae, lateral to the medial intertransversarii. Unlike the deep back muscles proper they belong to the anterior (hypaxial) muscles and are supplied by anterior rami. Each has a ventral and a dorsal part. They help bend the lumbar spine to the same side and steady its segments.
