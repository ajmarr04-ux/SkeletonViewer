The interosseous sacro-iliac ligament consists of very strong, short fibres that fill the irregular gap behind and above the sacro-iliac joint. It connects the tuberosity of the sacrum to the iliac tuberosity and is the main bond between the two bones, taking the weight of the trunk that passes from the sacrum to the hip bones.
