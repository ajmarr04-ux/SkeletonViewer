Pronator quadratus is a flat, square muscle across the front of the distal quarter of the radius and ulna, the deepest muscle of the front of the forearm. Its fibres run transversely from the ulna to the radius. It is the main pronator of the forearm, starting the movement, and its deep fibres hold the distal ends of the radius and ulna together.
