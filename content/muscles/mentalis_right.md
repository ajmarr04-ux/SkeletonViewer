Mentalis is a small conical muscle on each side of the frenulum of the lower lip. It arises from the incisive fossa of the mandible, below the incisor teeth, and passes downwards into the skin of the chin. It raises and wrinkles the skin of the chin and so protrudes and everts the lower lip, as in pouting, doubt or drinking.
