Orbicularis oris is the sphincter around the mouth, formed largely by fibres from bucinator and the other muscles that converge on the lips, together with its own fibres and slips from the incisive fossae of the maxilla and mandible. It closes the lips, presses them against the teeth and protrudes them, and shapes them for speech, whistling and sucking.
