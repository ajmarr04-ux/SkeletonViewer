The dorsal cuneonavicular ligaments are three bands on the upper surface of the midfoot. Each runs from the dorsal surface of the navicular bone to the dorsal surface of one of the three cuneiform bones, the medial, intermediate and lateral. They strengthen the cuneonavicular joint from above and hold the medial part of the tarsus together.
