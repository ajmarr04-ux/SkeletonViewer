The middle phalanx of the second toe is a short bone between the proximal and distal phalanges. Its base articulates with the head of the proximal phalanx at the proximal interphalangeal joint, and its head with the distal phalanx. Flexor digitorum brevis splits to insert on its sides, and the extensor tendons insert on its back.
