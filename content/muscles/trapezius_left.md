Trapezius is the large, flat, triangular muscle of the upper back and neck; with its fellow it forms a trapezium from the skull to the lower thoracic spine. It runs from the occipital bone, nuchal ligament and thoracic spinous processes to the clavicle and the spine of the scapula. Its three parts elevate, retract and depress the scapula, and together rotate it upwards when the arm is raised overhead.
