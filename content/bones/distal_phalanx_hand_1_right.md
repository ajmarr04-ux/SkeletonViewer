The distal phalanx of the thumb is the larger of the two terminal bones of the thumb. Its base articulates with the head of the proximal phalanx at the interphalangeal joint. The long flexor and long extensor of the thumb insert on its base, and its rough, flattened tuberosity supports the pulp and the nail at the tip.
