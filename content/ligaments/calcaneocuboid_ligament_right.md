The calcaneocuboid ligament is the band that joins the calcaneus to the cuboid across the lateral side of the foot. It strengthens the capsule of the calcaneocuboid joint, which forms the lateral part of the transverse tarsal joint. Its fibres help to hold the two bones together and to keep the lateral longitudinal arch of the foot in shape.
