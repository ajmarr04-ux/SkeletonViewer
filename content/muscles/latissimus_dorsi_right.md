Latissimus dorsi is the broad, flat muscle of the lower back. It arises from the spinous processes of the lower six thoracic vertebrae, the thoracolumbar fascia, the iliac crest and the lower three or four ribs, often with a slip from the inferior angle of the scapula. It converges, twisting, to the floor of the intertubercular sulcus of the humerus. It extends, adducts and medially rotates the arm, as in climbing and swimming.
