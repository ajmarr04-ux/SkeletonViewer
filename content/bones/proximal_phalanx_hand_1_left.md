The proximal phalanx of the thumb is the first of its two phalanges; the thumb has no middle phalanx. Its concave base articulates with the head of the first metacarpal, and its pulley-shaped head with the distal phalanx. The short muscles of the thumb insert around its base and steady the joint during pinch and grip.
