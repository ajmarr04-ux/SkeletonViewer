The four dorsal interossei fill the spaces between the metacarpals, each arising by two heads from the adjacent bones. They insert on the bases of the proximal phalanges and the extensor expansions: the first on the radial side of the index, the second and third on either side of the middle finger, and the fourth on the ulnar side of the ring finger. They abduct the fingers from the line of the middle finger.
