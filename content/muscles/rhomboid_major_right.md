Rhomboid major is a thin, quadrilateral sheet deep to trapezius. It arises from the spinous processes of T2 to T5 and the supraspinous ligament and runs down and laterally to the medial border of the scapula, from the root of the spine to the inferior angle. It retracts the scapula, rotates it so the glenoid faces down, and holds it against the chest wall.
