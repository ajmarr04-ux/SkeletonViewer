The posterior meniscotibial ligament of the lateral meniscus is a short fibrous band, sometimes called the posterior horn attachment. It fixes the back end of the lateral meniscus to the posterior intercondylar area of the tibia. It keeps the posterior horn in place on the tibial plateau, so that the meniscus stays correctly positioned as the femoral condyle rolls and glides across it.
