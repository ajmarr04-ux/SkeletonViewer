The sixth costal cartilage continues the front end of the sixth rib and runs medially to the lower part of the body of the sternum, where it forms a synovial sternocostal joint. It is a true-rib cartilage, so it joins the rib directly to the breastbone. Its elasticity allows the chest wall to expand and recoil in breathing.
