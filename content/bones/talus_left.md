The talus is the highest bone of the foot. Its body is gripped between the malleoli of the tibia and fibula, and its pulley-shaped upper surface makes the ankle joint. Below, it rests on the calcaneus; in front, its head articulates with the navicular. Much of its surface is covered with cartilage, and no muscles attach to it.
