The capitohamate interosseous ligament is a short, strong band between the capitate and hamate, in the distal row of the carpus. It lies between the adjacent surfaces of the two bones, near their distal ends. It binds them tightly, so that the distal carpal row moves almost as one unit and forms a stable base for the metacarpals.
