The sixth cervical vertebra is a typical cervical vertebra whose anterior tubercles are large and are called the carotid tubercles. The vertebral artery usually enters the transverse foramina here. Its level in the neck matches the cricoid cartilage, where the larynx becomes the trachea and the pharynx becomes the oesophagus.
