The interosseous membrane of the forearm is a strong fibrous sheet stretched between the interosseous borders of the radius and ulna. Most of its fibres run obliquely downwards and medially from the radius to the ulna. It links the two bones, passes load from the radius to the ulna and provides attachment for deep forearm muscles.
