The third rib is a typical rib: a curved, flattened bar with a head bearing two facets for T2 and T3, a neck, a tubercle that articulates with the transverse process of T3, an angle, and a shaft with a costal groove sheltering the intercostal vessels and nerve. Its cartilage joins the sternum.
