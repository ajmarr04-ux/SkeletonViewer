The hamate is a wedge-shaped bone at the little-finger end of the distal carpal row, recognised by the hook that curves forward from its palm surface. It articulates with the capitate, triquetrum and lunate, and below with the fourth and fifth metacarpals. The hook and the pisiform anchor the flexor retinaculum on the medial side of the carpal tunnel.
