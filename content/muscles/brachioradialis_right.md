Brachioradialis is the superficial muscle along the lateral side of the forearm, forming the lateral border of the cubital fossa. It arises from the upper lateral supracondylar ridge of the humerus and inserts at the base of the radial styloid process. Although it belongs to the extensor compartment and is supplied by the radial nerve, it flexes the elbow, most strongly with the forearm half pronated.
