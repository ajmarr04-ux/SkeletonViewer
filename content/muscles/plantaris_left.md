Plantaris is a small muscle with a short belly and a very long, thin tendon, absent in about one person in ten. It arises from the lower lateral supracondylar line of the femur and the oblique popliteal ligament, and its tendon runs between gastrocnemius and soleus to the calcaneus, beside the calcaneal tendon. It weakly assists plantarflexion and knee flexion.
