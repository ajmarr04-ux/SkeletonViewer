Extensor carpi radialis longus arises from the lower lateral supracondylar ridge of the humerus, just below brachioradialis. Its tendon runs along the radius, under the extensor retinaculum in the second dorsal compartment, to the back of the base of the second metacarpal. It extends and abducts (radially deviates) the wrist, and it steadies the wrist when the fingers grip.
