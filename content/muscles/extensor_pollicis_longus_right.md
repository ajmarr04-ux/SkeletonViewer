Extensor pollicis longus arises from the middle third of the back of the ulna and the interosseous membrane. Its tendon turns round the dorsal tubercle of the radius in its own compartment and forms the back border of the anatomical snuffbox, inserting on the base of the distal phalanx of the thumb. It extends all the joints of the thumb and is the only muscle that extends its interphalangeal joint strongly.
