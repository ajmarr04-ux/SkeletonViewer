Levator palpebrae superioris arises from the inferior surface of the lesser wing of the sphenoid, above and in front of the optic canal. It runs forwards above superior rectus and fans out into a broad aponeurosis that inserts into the skin of the upper eyelid and the superior tarsal plate. It raises the upper eyelid and holds it open.
