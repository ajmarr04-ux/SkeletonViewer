The diaphragm is the dome-shaped musculotendinous partition between the thoracic and abdominal cavities, and the chief muscle of breathing. Its sternal, costal and lumbar parts arise from the xiphoid process, the inner surfaces of the lower six costal cartilages and ribs, and the upper lumbar vertebrae by its crura and the arcuate ligaments, and converge on the central tendon. Contracting, it descends and enlarges the thorax.
