The anterior sternoclavicular ligament is a broad band covering the front of the sternoclavicular joint. It runs from the upper front of the sternal end of the clavicle downwards and medially to the front of the manubrium of the sternum. It reinforces the capsule and stops the medial end of the clavicle moving upwards and forwards.
