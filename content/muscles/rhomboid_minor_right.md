Rhomboid minor is a narrow band lying just above rhomboid major and often fused with it. It arises from the lower end of the nuchal ligament and the spinous processes of C7 and T1, and runs to the medial border of the scapula at the root of the spine. It acts with rhomboid major to retract the scapula, rotate it downwards and hold it to the chest wall.
