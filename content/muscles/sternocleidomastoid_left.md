Sternocleidomastoid is the prominent strap muscle that crosses the side of the neck obliquely, dividing it into anterior and posterior triangles. Its sternal head arises from the front of the manubrium, its clavicular head from the medial clavicle, and both run up to the mastoid process and superior nuchal line. Acting alone it tilts the head to its own side and turns the face to the opposite side.
