Opponens digiti minimi of the foot is an inconstant slip of deep fibres of flexor digiti minimi brevis, not recognised as a separate muscle in every text. It arises from the long plantar ligament near the base of the fifth metatarsal and inserts along the lateral border of that bone. It may draw the fifth metatarsal down and in.
