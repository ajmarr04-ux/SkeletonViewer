## Stop conditions (spec §2)

1. **Muscles not separated left/right: no.** Every paired muscle is two meshes with `.l` / `.r` names, and no side-named mesh crosses the midline: none found.
2. **A major muscle missing: no.** Checked against 58 major muscles (table below). All present.
3. **Parts that can't be matched to anatomy for a large share of muscles: no.** 54 left/midline part meshes, all named as an anatomical division (head, part, belly, tendon, aponeurosis) of a named muscle; listed below.
4. **Articular cartilage (plan risk 1): largely absent, as expected.** The source has no articular cartilage on any joint surface. What it has per joint is listed below. Under the plan, the missing articular cartilage is recorded as `missing` in the mapping, not modelled.

Conditions 1 to 3 don't hold, so the plan doesn't require a stop for them. **Condition 4 is the plan's own checkpoint: the user is asked before session Q** whether to go on without articular cartilage.

## Naming and side problems

Sides were read from the name suffix and checked against the centroid's x (patient's right is −X; |x| under 5 mm counts as midline, so small paired muscles near the midline, such as the interspinales or genioglossus, aren't flagged).

**Name says one side, geometry says the other** (session Q takes the side from geometry):
- `Lateral temporomandibular ligament.l`: centroid x = -0.0538 m (right)
- `Lateral temporomandibular ligament.r`: centroid x = +0.0538 m (left)

**No side suffix, but off the midline:**
- `Intra-articular ligament of head of rib`: left (x = +0.0169 m)
- `Ulnopisiform ligament`: right (x = -0.2414 m)
- `Iliocostalis colli muscle`: left (x = +0.0536 m)

So `Iliocostalis colli muscle`, `Intra-articular ligament of head of rib` and `Ulnopisiform ligament` are complete left/right pairs whose one mesh only lacks its suffix, and the `Lateral temporomandibular ligament` has its left and right names swapped. Session Q takes these sides from geometry. (Corrected in session Q: the first version of this report called two of them one-sided.)

## Decision on `2: Muscular insertions` (plan step 2, decision 12)

**Decision: usable.** 689 of 705 patches (98%) lie on a bone or cartilage surface: the median distance of their vertices to the nearest one is under 1 mm (typically 0.25 mm). Each is a low-poly sheet given Subdivision (level 1) and a 0.5 mm Solidify in the source, so a thin closed shell of 88 to 17020 triangles once evaluated (931,484 in all; 63,843 before modifiers), parented to the bone it sits on.

- **Names:** `<muscle or part name>.<o|e>[n]<l|r>`: `o` = origin (351), `e` = insertion (354), `n` numbers several patches of one end. All 705 names parse. 167 distinct muscle names; 150 match a muscle mesh or a source muscle group. Unmatched (session Q maps these by hand): (Abdominal part of pectoralis major muscle), (Opponens digiti minimi muscle of foot), Adductor pollicis, Articularis genus muscle, Biceps femoris muscle, Common extensor tendon, Common flexor tendon, Extensor carpi ulnaris, Flexor carpi ulnaris, Flexor digitorum superficialis, Lateral pterygoid muscle, Masseter, Patellar ligament, Pronator teres, Quadriceps femoris muscle, Triceps surae muscle, Trochanteric insertion.
- **Sides:** split left and right, but the suffix is **unreliable**: 50 patches lie on the other side from their suffix (for example both scalene, rectus capitis and serratus patches have `l`/`r` swapped). 7 name keys occur twice on the same side, which means the source has a right-side patch named `…l` and **no left-side twin** (serratus posterior superior and inferior, plantar interossei, pronator quadratus, piriformis, pectineus and others). So: side from geometry, and the missing left twins go in `missing` (not mirrored).
- **Not on a surface (16):** `Abductor pollicis longus.ol` (3.3 mm from Ulna.l); `Abductor pollicis longus.or` (3.3 mm from Ulna.r); `Diaphragm.ol` (14.1 mm from Costal cartilage of seventh rib.l); `Diaphragm.or` (14.1 mm from Costal cartilage of seventh rib.r); `Extensor hallucis longus.ol` (4.3 mm from Fibula.l); `Extensor hallucis longus.or` (4.3 mm from Fibula.r); `Extensor pollicis brevis.ol` (2.6 mm from Radius.l); `Extensor pollicis brevis.or` (2.6 mm from Radius.r); `External abdominal oblique muscle.o5l` (16.9 mm from Hip bone.l); `External abdominal oblique muscle.o5r` (16.9 mm from Hip bone.r); `Internal abdominal oblique muscle.o1l` (6.0 mm from Costal cartilage of tenth rib.l); `Internal abdominal oblique muscle.o1r` (6.0 mm from Costal cartilage of tenth rib.r); `Rectus abdominis muscle.el` (143.8 mm from Hip bone.l); `Rectus abdominis muscle.er` (143.8 mm from Hip bone.r); `Tibialis posterior muscle.ol` (8.2 mm from Fibula.l); `Tibialis posterior muscle.or` (8.2 mm from Fibula.r). Most span two bones and the membrane between them (abductor pollicis longus, tibialis posterior, extensor hallucis longus, extensor pollicis brevis) or several costal cartilages (diaphragm, internal oblique), so they are real and only the 1 mm test is too strict for them. **`Rectus abdominis muscle.e*` is misplaced** (144 mm from any bone), and its `o` patches sit on the costal cartilages, where the muscle inserts, so its origin and insertion labels look swapped.
- **On meshes that aren't bones:** Costal cartilage of fifth rib.l (1), Costal cartilage of fifth rib.r (1), Costal cartilage of seventh rib.l (2), Costal cartilage of seventh rib.r (2), Costal cartilage of sixth rib.l (1), Costal cartilage of sixth rib.r (1), Costal cartilage of tenth rib.l (1), Costal cartilage of tenth rib.r (1), Sesamoid bones of foot.l (2), Sesamoid bones of foot.r (2), Thyroid cartilage (2). The costal cartilages become cartilage-layer objects, so these patches still have a structure to sit on; the thyroid cartilage and the hallux sesamoids are excluded, so their patches are dropped.
- **Consequence:** origin and insertion in the content (M8) come from references, never from the patch names; the patches are geometry only. Session Q maps each kept patch to `<muscle or part id>__origin|__insertion[_n]` under `attachments`, and session R exports them as `attachments-{high,low}.glb` (plan decision 12). M9 then draws them on the bones (spec §5.3).

- **Modifiers:** SUBSURF ×1073, SOLIDIFY ×1062, WIREFRAME ×2. Muscles are mostly plain meshes; the ligaments and patches are low-poly sheets with Subdivision level 1 (render level 2) and Solidify 0.1–2 mm. All figures here use the **viewport** evaluation, as the bone build does (`evaluated_depsgraph_get`); the render levels would multiply the subdivided meshes' triangles by about 4. Session R evaluates the same way, so its bounds check matches this audit.
- Modifiers switched off in the viewport but on for render: Solidify on `Plantar aponeurosis` and `Superficial transverse metatarsal ligament` (both doubtful; if kept, they would export as unthickened sheets unless session R enables the modifier), Wireframe on the excluded `Lateral femoral intermuscular septum`, Subdivision on the excluded `Common flexor tendon sheath.l`.
- 36 kept meshes have non-manifold edges (listed per mesh in the JSON, `nonManifoldEdges`); the build closes small holes and logs what remains, as for the bones.
- 530 soft-tissue meshes have a negative world scale (left-side linked duplicates, as for the bones), so the build makes the mesh data single-user and flips winding, as `build_skeleton.py` does.
