Mylohyoid is a flat, triangular sheet that, with its fellow, forms the muscular floor of the mouth. It arises from the whole length of the mylohyoid line of the mandible; its fibres meet those of the other side in a midline raphe, and the posterior fibres reach the body of the hyoid. It supports and raises the floor of the mouth and the tongue in swallowing.
