The sixth thoracic vertebra is a typical thoracic vertebra. Its facet joints face mostly backwards, allowing rotation but little bending, and its long spinous process overlaps the vertebra below. Demifacets on its body take the heads of ribs 6 and 7, and its transverse costal facets take the tubercles of rib 6.
