Opponens pollicis is a small quadrilateral muscle lying deep to abductor and flexor pollicis brevis. It arises from the flexor retinaculum and the tubercle of the trapezium and inserts along the whole lateral border of the first metacarpal. It rotates and draws the first metacarpal across the palm at the carpometacarpal joint, so that the pad of the thumb meets the pads of the fingers.
