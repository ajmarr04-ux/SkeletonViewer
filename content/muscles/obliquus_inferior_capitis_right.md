Obliquus capitis inferior is the larger of the two oblique muscles and, despite its name, the only suboccipital muscle with no attachment to the skull. It runs laterally and upwards from the spinous process of the axis to the transverse process of the atlas, forming the lower side of the suboccipital triangle. It turns the atlas, and with it the face, to its own side.
