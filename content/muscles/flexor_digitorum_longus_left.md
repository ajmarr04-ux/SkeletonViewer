Flexor digitorum longus arises from the back of the tibia below the soleal line. Its tendon passes behind the medial malleolus, crosses the tendon of flexor hallucis longus in the sole, receives quadratus plantae and divides into four tendons to the bases of the distal phalanges of the second to fifth toes. It flexes these toes and helps plantarflex the ankle.
