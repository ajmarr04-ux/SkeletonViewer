Coracobrachialis is a slender muscle in the upper medial arm. It arises from the tip of the coracoid process with the short head of biceps and inserts on the middle of the medial border of the humerus. It helps flex and adduct the arm and resists downward dislocation of the shoulder. The musculocutaneous nerve usually pierces it, which is a useful landmark.
