The pisotriquetral ligament is a thin band of fibres around the small joint between the pisiform and the palmar surface of the triquetrum. It strengthens the joint capsule, which is usually separate from the other wrist joints. Because the pisiform lies within the tendon of flexor carpi ulnaris, the ligament helps to hold that bone in place as the tendon pulls on it.
