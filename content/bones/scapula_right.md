The scapula is a flat, triangular bone on the back of the chest wall, covering ribs 2 to 7. A spine across its back ends in the acromion, the point of the shoulder. Its shallow glenoid cavity receives the head of the humerus, and the coracoid process juts forward beneath the clavicle. Seventeen muscles attach to it.
